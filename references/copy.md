# Copy on glass

The deck argues with devices, not sentences. CLAUDE.md §4 governs everything here; this is how it reads on a slide.

## Titles
- One claim the slide proves, 14 words or fewer, sentence case, no full stop. "Three agents share one governed data
  foundation", never "Our Solution" or "Architecture Overview".
- One to three keywords of the claim in the brand role (`text.title(keywords=...)`), in titles only.
- No subtitle. A sentence that wants to sit under the title is either the title or a speaker note.

## Card bodies
- A heading of one to four words, then one or two lines. A body that needs a paragraph belongs in the notes deck.
- Every card in a row carries the same number of rows, so no card is stretched around one line.
- Say the fact: "Owner  medical reviewer" beats a sentence about who owns it. Label-value rows carry more than prose
  in a third of the reading time.
- Commas, full stops or the deck's own middot. A semicolon on a slide is the tell; colon-then-list is too.

## Voice
- No first or second person on glass (we, our, you, your). The survivors are a quotation and "Thank you".
- The audience's own vocabulary. Keep the project's `present` and `absent` lists beside the build.
- No narration: no "This slide shows", no reading instructions, no explanation of why a feature exists, no citing the
  client's own research back at them.
- American spelling unless the project rules otherwise (the offerings folder is British).
- Never describe the firm's own work as an alpha, prototype, demo or POC in sales copy; those words are fine when
  they are the client's own vocabulary.

## Numbers
- Every figure is a `prims.stat` with a kind: measured, delivered, illustrative, target or benchmark. The last three
  print their chip.
- Round, and anchor a range to a stated baseline. Precision is itself a fingerprint.
- Approved firm claims only (CLAUDE.md §5): "100+ AI products delivered", "20+ production-ready agents".

## Speaker notes
- A separate presenter-only deck, never in the deck that travels (gate P20).
- A verbatim transcript: no stage directions, no "SAY:", no colour names (a client cut may not have that colour).

## Before rewriting copy in volume
Count first (semicolons, runs at or below 9pt, words per slide against the budget), then judge each item against
one bar: cut what restates the label above it; keep what states a fact the label does not. Report what went and
what stayed.
