# Principles

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a06 | f02 | catalogue | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience must agree with how the work will be done before seeing what will be built, and the stance breaks into a handful of peer beliefs or design decisions.
- Each principle can be stated as a short heading, often a contrast such as one thing before another, with a rationale of one or two lines.
- Each principle can be proven by its own small visual, a mini diagram or a screen, so the column carries both the rule and its evidence.

## Avoid when
- The items are reasons to choose the vendor, use why-us (a07) instead.
- The items are parts of the offer a client would buy, use offering-pillars (a08) instead.
- The items are steps that run in order, use process-flow (a13) instead.
- The point is the problem the principles answer, use problem-framing (a05) instead.

## Components
- Peer cards or rows in a repeat of 3 (2 to 4), each card 28% (22 to 46) of the slide width and 35% (9 to 67) of its height.
- Icons per slide 4 (0 to 11), one pictogram per principle at most.
- Connectors per slide 0 (0 to 13), only inside a mini diagram or as leaders from a numbered disc.
- An ordinal per principle, a disc or a large numeral set at 14.75 pt (12 to 26.75).

## Density
Text shapes per slide 19 (16 to 29). The card zone fills 73% (29 to 76) of the slide height and 92% (61 to 95) of its width, so the grid is the governing visual.

## Word budget
Word budget 250, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 135 (93 to 340).

## Device notes
- Every principle carries an ordinal, a disc or a large numeral at the top left of its card or row (d01-s086, d13-s009, d12-s056, d13-s028).
- One pictogram per principle sits between heading and rationale, never a second decorative icon (d01-s086, d13-s009, d13-s028).
- Headings state the principle as a contrast or an imperative, one thing before another or one thing and not another, and the body gives only the reason (d01-s024, d13-s009, d01-s086).
- A column ends or begins in its own proof, a mini diagram or a product screen, so the claim and its evidence share the column (d01-s024, d13-s028).

## Variants
- A numbered card grid, heading over pictogram over reason (d01-s086).
- Numbered cards that split need from design answer under a hairline (d13-s009).
- Question columns, each with a mini diagram over its principle cards (d01-s024).
- Numbered rows on an arc around one anchoring visual (d12-s056).
- Numbered theme columns under tab headers, each ending in a screen (d13-s028).
