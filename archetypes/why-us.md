# Why us

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a07 | f02 | claim-proof | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience is choosing between vendors and needs a few reasons it can repeat afterwards, each backed by a short proof.
- Each reason has its own evidence, a payoff line, a sector reference or a concrete asset, so the reasons argue rather than list.
- A shared set of assets or outside recognitions backs every reason at once and can sit as one band under the pillars.

## Avoid when
- The slide carries scale figures and recognitions alone with no reasons, use credentials (a43) instead.
- The reasons are arranged around a central mark, use hub-and-spoke (a34) instead.
- Every service is listed with equal weight and nothing argues, use catalogue (a09) instead.
- The items are parts of the offer the client would buy, use offering-pillars (a08) instead.

## Components
- Numbered pillars, cards or rows in a repeat of 4 (3 to 5), each 22% (17 to 72) of the slide width and 50% (7 to 56) of its height.
- Icons per slide 6 (3 to 12), one per pillar header and one per enabler cell.
- Logos per slide 0 (0 to 0), which the house replaces with sector and scale descriptors.
- A large ordinal per reason set at 18 pt (12 to 24).

## Density
Text shapes per slide 25 (10 to 28). The pillar zone fills 48% (46 to 50) of the slide height and 92% (91 to 93) of its width.

## Word budget
Word budget 210, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 184 (146 to 227).

## Device notes
- Every reason carries a large ordinal on or beside its header, sized to be read before the header text (d01-s026, d01-s027, d16-s023).
- Each pillar header pairs one icon with a short bold label, and the body under it is the proof, not a restatement (d01-s026, d01-s027).
- A full-width band under the pillars names what backs all of them, shared assets or outside recognitions, at 71% (71 to 71) of the slide height (d01-s026, d01-s027).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| first-person | Titles, foot strips and claim lines spoken in the first person, on 1 top exemplar and on d16-s023. | Reasons stated in the third person as claims about the vendor. | Claim lines grow slightly, so cut each pillar body to two lines. |
| glyphs | Curly apostrophes and spaced hyphens used as dashes, on 1 top exemplar and on d16-s023. | Straight apostrophes, commas and colons. | None on geometry. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar that encodes no state, on 2 top exemplars. | No accent bar unless it encodes a state that can change. | The title starts at the shared left margin. |
| no-footer | Wordmarks, confidentiality lines, gradient foot bands and a closing callout band, on 2 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The callout band is dropped and its point moves into the title, which returns its height to the pillars. |
| no-subtitle | A line under the title, on 1 top exemplar. | Fold it into the title claim. | The pillars start directly under the title. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows under the cards and the band, on 1 top exemplar. | Hairline outlines plus at most a 1 px shadow. | None on geometry. |
| flat-fill | Gradient numbered panels on d16-s023. | One flat brand tint behind the ordinal. | The ordinal carries the order on its own. |
| word-budget | Content words run 184 (146 to 227) across the top exemplars. | Hold the archetype budget of 210, never above 250. | Each pillar body becomes two lines plus its proof line. |
| other | Client logos as proof inside the cards, on 1 top exemplar, and titles of 8 (4 to 12) words. | Sector and scale descriptors in place of logos, and one claim of 14 words or fewer with up to 3 keywords in the brand color. | The logo row becomes a short text line at the foot of each card. |
