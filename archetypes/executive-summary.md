# Executive summary

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a28 | f05 | one-pager | v1 |

## Use when
- A reader who sees one page must be able to agree to the whole proposal: the ask, the answer, the scope, the plan and the assumptions each get a labelled cell.
- A response must show every requested item beside its counterpart, a request rail against an answer grid, so nothing in the ask reads as unanswered.

## Avoid when
- The page explains one use case or mechanism in depth, so use deep-dive instead.
- The page only fences what is in and out and what the offer rests on, so use scope-and-assumptions instead.
- The page prices the work and sets terms, so use commercials instead.
- The page shows a current to future shift with no plan or scope beneath it, so use from-to-shift instead.

## Components
- Answer cells, 4 (2 to 5) items in the dominant repeat, each under a named header and each holding one kind of content.
- Connectors 0 (0 to 2) per slide, used only where the bridge or the plan carries direction.
- Icons 7 (0 to 16) per slide, one leading each cell heading, in one line family.
- A plan strip of phases, each carrying its duration, and a band of assumption cards closing the page.

## Density
Content words 264 (5 to 380) across 5 top exemplars, with 29 (1 to 44) text shapes per slide. Every cell is cut to keywords to hold the budget.

## Word budget
Budget 250 words normalized and 250 on the w10 canvas, held at the ceiling because the top exemplars run long, advisory floor 160, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- Filled header bars name every cell, so the page reads as a labelled grid: ask against response, or plan, proof and results side by side.
- The answer splits into peer columns or quadrants of equal width, each led by a bold heading and an icon, each holding one kind of content.
- A band along the bottom of the answer bounds the offer, as assumption cards, a maturity ladder of delivery stages or the team mix by role.
- Every phase in the plan carries its duration beside it, as a pill under a chevron, a column in a phase table or a tick on a dated track.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| word-budget | Paragraph cells and full bullet columns push content words to or over the budget on 3 top exemplars. | Per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Each cell holds keywords or one line per item, and full scope lists move to an appendix page. |
| 8pt-floor | Ask bodies, stat captions and phase table cells run under the floor on 3 top exemplars. | No run under 8 pt. | Cells grow to the floor, so each ask body and caption is cut to one line and the activity column to keywords. |
| no-subtitle | A lead line, lead sentence or objective band sits under the title on 3 top exemplars. | No subtitle under the title. | The objective folds into the one-claim title, and the ask cell carries the request itself. |
| first-person | Titles and headers speak as our proposal, who we are or our response on 4 top exemplars. | No first or second person. | Headers become neutral labels such as response, offer and differentiators, and the title states a third-person claim. |
| glyphs | Arrow glyphs in point text, smart quotes or curly apostrophes appear on 4 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash or arrow glyph in text. | Flows read with the word to or become drawn connectors, and quotes are straight. |
| shadow | Soft blurred shadows lift header bars, tiles and quadrant cards on 3 top exemplars. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Cells keep hairline outlines only. |
| flat-fill | Gradient header bars, a faded arrow illustration behind the body or a gradient band appear on 3 top exemplars. | Flat fills, no gradients. | Header bars take one flat brand fill and the background illustration goes, since the bridge block carries direction alone. |
| no-footer | A page number, corner vendor mark, logo lockup or appendix note sits at the foot on 3 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The assumption band takes the bottom edge as content, with nothing under it. |
| left-accent | Colored left bars on stat tiles or a short title edge bar decorate the slide on 2 top exemplars. | A colored left or top accent bar only where it encodes a changeable state. | A hairline above each stat caption replaces the bars. |
| other | A summary flattened to one raster, an empty black band, a client mark in the team row or a quoted phrase in the brand color appear on 3 top exemplars. | Native shapes and text throughout, teams named by role, and a title of one claim in at most 14 words with at most 3 keywords in the brand color. | Every cell is rebuilt as editable text that the gates can measure, and party marks give way to role labels. |
