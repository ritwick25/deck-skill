## Use when
- The audience must approve a spend, so the price, range or cap has to sit beside the scope, team, duration and terms it buys.
- An itemised cost or a set of term blocks needs to resolve into one or two headline prices the approver can quote.

## Avoid when
- The figures are value to the client rather than the price of the work, so use headline-figures instead.
- Scope and assumptions are listed with no price attached, so use scope-and-assumptions instead.

## Components
- Dominant repeat of cards or tiles, 2 (2 to 4) items at 18% (15 to 21) of the canvas width.
- Icons 3 (1 to 7) per slide, on the price panel and on each term or scope item.
- Connectors 0 (0 to 0) per slide, as rules inside the price rail, a bracket or a fan of wedges.
- One price panel holding the headline figure, range or cap, set apart from the blocks that say what it buys.

## Density
Content words 74 (46 to 103) across 4 exemplars, title words 4 (1 to 4).

## Word budget
Budget 110 words normalized and 110 on the w10 canvas, advisory floor 40 and 40, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- The price sits in one dedicated panel or full-height rail at one side of the slide, apart from what it buys.
- An icon disc crowns the price panel at or across its top edge.
- The headline figure sits in a pill or capsule inside the price panel, one pill per price line.
- Each term or scope item carries its own icon, so duration, team and included work read as tagged blocks.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A confidentiality line, page number or corner logo lockup closes the slide on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The foot rows are cut and the price panel and term blocks take the full height. |
| shadow | Soft blurred shadows sit under price cards, cards or the price rail on 4 top exemplars. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | The price panel and cards take a hairline edge only. |
| glyphs | En dashes in price ranges, smart quotes in offer names or bullet and star glyphs in labels on 3 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash, arrow glyph or smart quote in text. | Price ranges read with the word to, so the price pill needs width for the longer span. |
| left-accent | Static edge bars on cards or at the slide edges mark no state on 3 top exemplars. | An accent bar only where it encodes a state that can change. | The bars go and a hairline above each label groups the term and scope blocks. |
| 8pt-floor | A footer label runs below the floor on 1 top exemplar. | Every run at 8 pt or above. | The label leaves with the footer, so no run needs resizing. |
| emoji | A decorative star glyph reads as an emoji in a band label on 1 top exemplar. | No emoji. | Band labels carry words only. |
| word-budget | Repeated layer labels in an itemised table push words past the budget on 1 top exemplar. | A per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Repeated labels merge into one spanning cell per group, which shortens the table. |
| first-person | Terms text speaks as we on 1 top exemplar. | No first or second person. | Terms name the vendor and the client by role. |
| light-bg | Saturated brand panels carry the price and term text on 1 top exemplar. | Light slide ground. The price and term panels stay solid brand fills with text in the on-accent role. | No change to geometry. Price and term keep reversed type on the solid panel. |
| other | Changes outside the named rules appear on 4 top exemplars, among them label titles, a brace typed as a character and cards nested in cards. | The title is one claim of 14 words or fewer, brackets are drawn and touch what they join, and no card sits inside another. | Titles tie the price to its outcome, the outer backing panel goes and the brace is redrawn as a shape. |
