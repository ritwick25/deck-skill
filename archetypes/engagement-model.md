# Engagement model

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a19 | f03 | sequence | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience must trust the way the engagement will run: the method steps or workstreams in order, with the activities and deliverables of each.
- Each step needs its own named outcome or gate, so the buyer can see what is handed over before the next step starts.

## Avoid when
- The slide shows how the solution itself processes work rather than how the engagement runs, so use process-flow instead.
- The phases sit on dates or a milestone rail, so use phased-roadmap instead.
- The point is which actor acts at each stage of the solution and where control passes, so use swimlane-handoff instead.
- The tiers are forums, cadences or decision rights rather than method steps, so use governance-model instead.

## Components
- Step markers in the dominant repeat, 3 (2 to 4) per slide, a numbered disc, a header tab or a filled panel per step, each with a short heading.
- Connectors 3 (0 to 7) per slide, one continuous track or short arrows between steps, each starting on one step and landing on the next.
- Icons 5 (1 to 15) per slide, one line glyph per step header at most.
- A short activity list under each step, aligned to the step column, with one named deliverable or outcome closing it.

## Density
Content words 153 (134 to 206) across 5 top exemplars, with 27 (24 to 45) text shapes per slide.

## Word budget
Budget 160 words normalized and 180 on the w13 canvas, advisory floor 140, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- Steps carry a number in a disc on or beside the step, so the method reads in order and each step can be cited by its number.
- A long sequence snakes across two or three rows along one continuous track, so the order follows the line without an arrow per step.
- Step headers sit as tabs or panels in one row, and the activity and outcome tiers below align to their column seams, so every activity reads under its step.
- Each step closes on a named outcome, an outcome cell, a pill or a gate card, so the deliverable is visible per step rather than listed at the end.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A logo lockup, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band appears on 5 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The step track and its outcome tier take the reclaimed height, and any foot claim moves into the title or the speaker notes. |
| no-subtitle | A strapline or lead sentence sits under the title on 3 top exemplars. | No subtitle under the title. | The line folds into the one-claim title or moves to the speaker notes, and the step track moves up. |
| first-person | The title speaks in the first person or a possessive on 2 top exemplars. | No first or second person. | Restate the title as a third-person claim about how the engagement runs. |
| glyphs | En dashes, smart quotes or an ellipsis appear in step headings on 4 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash or arrow glyph in text. | Write ranges with the word to and use a colon or straight quotes in headings. |
| 8pt-floor | Gate card labels and in-icon tags render below the floor on 1 top exemplar. | Every run at 8 pt or above. | Drop tags inside icons and widen the gate card so its labels reach the floor. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows sit under step discs or the base layer on 2 top exemplars. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Use a hairline ring or a flat light fill on each disc and drop the shadow. |
| flat-fill | Takeaway bands and edge bars carry gradients or partial transparency on 2 top exemplars. | Flat fills, no gradients. | Flat fills only, and gradient bars removed since they carry no content. |
| left-accent | A colored edge bar marks the title as decoration on 1 top exemplar. | A colored left or top accent bar only where it encodes a changeable state. | Remove the bar, the title stands on the light ground alone. |
| light-bg | A dark summary band closes the slide on 1 top exemplar. | Light slide grounds. | Cut the band with the foot band rule, or set any surviving summary on a light tint with a hairline. |
| other | A label title in place of a claim, a navigation roundel, a review tag pill or clip art outcome images appear on 4 top exemplars. | A title of one claim, no navigation or review chrome, one icon style. | Hold the title to 14 words with up to 3 keywords in the brand color, drop navigation and review chrome, and use one consistent line icon per outcome. |
