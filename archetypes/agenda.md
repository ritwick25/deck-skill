# Agenda

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a02 | f01 | agenda | v1 |

## Use when
- The session or document has a fixed set of parts and the audience should hold the whole route once, in order, before the first part starts.
- A workshop day runs in timed sessions, so the plan splits into session blocks with a break band and a duration column.

## Avoid when
- The same contents grid returns later with one part lit to mark the turn, so use section-divider (a03) instead.
- The list is a set of deliverables or workstreams rather than the order of the session, so use engagement-model or catalogue instead.
- The page opens the deck and names the parties and the date, so use cover (a01) instead.

## Components
- A short page title over the parts, often one word.
- Numbered parts, items per page 4 (2 to 10), each item 45% (40 to 71) wide and 13% (4 to 35) tall.
- A large numeral leads every part, with the part name beside or under it and one line of scope.
- Hairline rules between rows, gap between rows 3% (1 to 7).
- Optional session blocks spanning their rows, a duration column and a full-width break band for a day plan.
- Optional unnumbered appendix row set on the label column under the numbered parts.

## Density
Content words 56 (21 to 144) per slide, text shapes 13 (10 to 43). The parts fill the canvas under a short title, title words 1 (1 to 10).

## Word budget
Word budget 70 with an advisory floor of 40, under the hard ceiling of 250. The title stays within 14 words.

## Device notes
- A large numeral leads every part and carries the order on its own (d02-s002, d16-s003, d13-s002, d10-s002, d14-s012).
- Four parts sit as a two-by-two tile grid with a giant numeral at the left of each tile (d02-s002, d16-s003).
- Each part carries a heading over a short scope line or a few points (d02-s002, d16-s003, d10-s002).
- Rows are separated by rules rather than by boxes or space alone (d13-s002, d10-s002, d14-s012).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| flat-fill | Gradient tiles, cards and session blocks on 3 top exemplars. | Flat brand fill for the active or lead part, flat light gray for the rest. | Fill becomes the only state carrier, so heading size and alignment stay identical across parts. |
| left-accent | Coloured rail bars on every row and a gradient edge bar on 2 top exemplars. | No decorative rails. The current part is marked by numeral color or fill only. | Rows rely on the numeral and a hairline for rhythm. |
| shadow | Soft shadows under panels and the active tile on 2 top exemplars. | Hairline outline plus at most a 1 px shadow. | Panels sit flat on the ground and read as rows of one list. |
| first-person | Headings and points written in the first person on 2 top exemplars. | Neutral noun phrases such as the brief and the response. | Part names get shorter and fit one line. |
| glyphs | En and em dashes in time ranges and descriptors, curly quotes and bullet markers on 3 top exemplars. | Ranges written with to, a colon in place of a dash, plain stacked lines without bullet glyphs. | Scope lines lose their markers and need a consistent indent. |
| no-footer | A foot strip with a confidentiality label and a wordmark on 2 top exemplars. | No footer, no confidentiality line, no page number. | The parts may run lower on the canvas. |
| 8pt-floor | A footer label set below the floor on 1 top exemplar. | Every run at or above 8 pt. | The label leaves with the footer. |
| no-subtitle | An agenda line under an event title on 1 top exemplar. | One title, no line under it. | Venue and date move into the column header or out of the page. |
| other | Stock photos beside the list, mixed heading alignment and loose numeral edges on 4 top exemplars. | The parts take the full width, and every numeral, label and rule snaps to one left edge and one row pitch. | The list becomes the whole page and its alignment is the device. |

## Variants
- Numbered rows on hairlines, the default for five or more parts (d13-s002).
- Numbered panels with a heading and one-line scope per part (d10-s002).
- Two-by-two numbered tile grid for four parts, reusable as the divider frame (d02-s002, d16-s003).
- Day plan with session blocks, a duration column and a break band (d14-s012).
