# Value horizons

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a39 | f08 | roadmap | v1 |

## Use when
- Value, capability or a relationship grows across years or horizons, and the slide must show both the climb and the destination.
- The operating change per horizon matters as much as the figure, so small sketches ride the climb beside the value each step adds.

## Avoid when
- The content is a dated delivery schedule with bars or phase scope, so use gantt or phased-roadmap instead.
- Levels of capability are ranked with no time scale, so use maturity-model instead.

## Components
- Dominant repeat of drawn shapes, one per horizon, 4 (2 to 4) items, with a gutter of 3% (0 to 11) of the canvas width.
- Icons 31 (0 to 82) per slide, person and agent glyphs inside vignettes and icon discs beside value callouts.
- Connectors 13 (0 to 16) per slide, dashed year guides, flow arrows inside vignettes and strip rules.
- One rising line or step outline across the full width, with a ring marker or disc at each horizon.

## Device notes
- Year chevrons step in a tonal ramp from pale to solid, so the last horizon reads as the destination.
- A rising curve carries one ring marker per stage, and vignettes or callouts sit at those markers.
- Small operating vignettes of person and agent glyphs show human steps giving way to agent steps from horizon to horizon.
- Each step or phase card sits one level higher than the one before it, so position alone shows the climb.
- A row of rising bars under the climb restates cumulative value as a second encoding.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| glyphs | En dashes in figure ranges, em dashes or arrow glyphs on 5 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash, arrow glyph or smart quote in text. | Ranges are written with the word to, so value callouts need a wider slot or one figure each. |
| flat-fill | Gradient area fills under the curve, gradient arrows or gradient label tiles on 4 top exemplars. | Flat fills, no gradients. | The area under the curve takes one flat tint, and the axis becomes a hairline with end labels. |
| no-footer | A full-width delivers strip, wordmark or confidentiality line closes the slide on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The outcome strip folds into the last horizon or the notes, and the climb field takes its height. |
| left-accent | A gradient edge bar or title edge bar on 3 top exemplars. | An accent bar only where it encodes a state that can change. | The edge bar goes, and the current horizon is marked by its curve marker instead. |
| shadow | Soft shadows on phase cards or the delivers strip on 3 top exemplars. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Cards and strips take a hairline outline only. |
| word-budget | Curve callouts, bar labels and strip text run past the budget on 3 top exemplars. | A per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | One value encoding stays, curve callouts or the bar row, and callouts drop to one line each. |
| 8pt-floor | Item rows inside the phase cards run below the floor on 1 top exemplar. | Every run at 8 pt or above. | Cards keep item names only and descriptions move to the notes. |
| first-person | The title and a step heading speak as we or our on 1 top exemplar. | No first or second person. | The title becomes one third-person claim about the climb. |
| light-bg | A photo under a dark overlay carries the enabler rail on 1 top exemplar. | Light slide grounds. | The side rail becomes a light tint panel with dark ink. |
| other | Changes outside the named rules appear on 5 top exemplars, among them two-line titles of up to 22 (6 to 22) words and placeholder figures. | The title is one claim of 14 words or fewer with at most 3 brand keywords. | The title shrinks to one line, which lifts the horizon header and gives the climb more height. |
