# Catalogue

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a09 | f02 | catalogue | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience needs to scan every agent, product or accelerator on offer, and each entry can carry the same fields, such as name, type, purpose and a link.
- The set is large enough that uniform rows or tiles beat prose, and grouping by function, stage or component helps the eye find an entry.
- Each entry can be closed by a concrete action or a tiny flow, a link to a live demo or an input to output strip, rather than a paragraph.

## Avoid when
- The entries need to sit on the client's value chain or phases to show coverage, use use-case-map (a30) instead.
- Each entry carries many attribute columns that must be compared cell by cell, use reference-table (a33) instead.
- Only a few parts are offered and each one is something to buy with a shared outcome, use offering-pillars (a08) instead.
- One product should be shown running rather than listed, use product-screen (a44) instead.

## Components
- Entries in a dominant repeat of 6 (4 to 16), each 22% (12 to 30) of the slide width and 8% (2 to 14) of its height.
- Icons per slide 7 (0 to 27), one per entry at its left or in its header, never decorative.
- Pictures per slide 16 (0 to 27), all icon or lockup sized, no screenshots.
- Entry stacks separated by a vertical gutter of 2% (1 to 4) of the slide height, so rows read on a fixed pitch.

## Density
Text shapes per slide 38 (20 to 89). The entry zone fills 74% (27 to 79) of the slide height and 92% (60 to 94) of its width.

## Word budget
Word budget 210, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 167 (140 to 354).

## Device notes
- Every entry repeats one field order, an icon or disc, a bold name, a short type tag and a one or two line purpose, so the set scans down a column (d01-s030, d04-s007, d18-s023, d01-s033).
- Solid header bars name the function, stage or agent above each column of entries at 20% (16 to 21) of the slide height (d04-s007, d02-s005, d01-s033).
- Rows inside a column are split by dashed gray hairlines rather than boxed one by one (d01-s030, d01-s033).
- One small mark, a star or a lettered disc, types a class of entry across the whole set and is decoded once in a short legend near the foot (d04-s007, d01-s033).
- A full-width band under the entries names the shared platform or the outcome each group buys (d04-s007, d01-s033).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8pt-floor | Flow strip labels under each column set below the floor, on 1 top exemplar. | Cut each strip label to two or three words at the floor or above | The strip grows taller, so the column text above it loses a line. |
| first-person | A possessive title opener and a column that speaks as we, on 2 top exemplars. | Neutral nouns naming the assets, not their owner | None on geometry. |
| glyphs | An en dash inside the pipeline line, on 1 top exemplar. | A colon in its place | None on geometry. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar running the slide height, on 2 top exemplars. | No accent bar, since it encodes no state | Title and entries start at the shared left margin. |
| no-footer | Confidentiality lines, wordmarks, page numbers, footnotes and gradient foot bands, on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot band, and a pipeline note becomes an entry or is cut | The shared band or the last entry row sits on the bottom margin. |
| no-subtitle | A sentence under the title restating the scope and the count, on 2 top exemplars. | The count folds into the title claim | The group headers move up into the freed band. |
| other | Filled orange link buttons and a repeated count in every card header, on 2 top exemplars. | Hairline outlined link chips, and the header count dropped | The action column stops outweighing the purpose text. |
| shadow | Soft drop shadows under every component card, on 1 top exemplar. | Hairline outlines with at most a 1 px shadow | None on geometry. |
| word-budget | Content words above the budget, with long purpose lines and method tags, on 3 top exemplars. | Each purpose cut to one short line and method tags dropped | Rows per column fall to three or four, and the full list moves to an appendix. |

## Variants
- Ruled register rows ending in link chips (d01-s030).
- Function columns of name and purpose tiles over a platform band (d04-s007).
- Grouped component cards of role over tool pairs (d18-s023).
- Agent columns each closing on an input to output strip (d02-s005).
- Stage columns of lettered entries over outcome blocks (d01-s033).
