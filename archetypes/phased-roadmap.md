# Phased roadmap

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a38 | f08 | roadmap | v1 |

## Use when
- Delivery breaks into a few phases or waves in time order, and each phase must show the same anatomy of scope, deliverables, exit and duration.
- The audience needs to see where the work stands now and what the next phase hands over, on one milestone rail.

## Avoid when
- Workstreams overlap on a dated week or month grid and the claim is about duration or a critical date, so use gantt instead.
- The slide asks the audience for decisions now rather than showing the delivery sequence, so use the-ask instead.
- Value grows across years with no delivery scope per phase, so use value-horizons instead.

## Components
- Dominant repeat of drawn shapes, phase cards or flow boxes, 5 (2 to 5) items, with a gutter of 4% (2 to 6) of the canvas width between them.
- Connectors 7 (6 to 22) per slide, the milestone rail, handoff arrows between phases and fan-out lines to deliverables.
- Icons 7 (0 to 12) per slide, on rail nodes, deliverable rows or requirement pairs.
- Phase headers as chevrons, banner arrows or bracket labels, one per phase, each carrying the duration.

## Density
Content words 159 (106 to 244) across 5 exemplars, title words 10 (4 to 21).

## Word budget
Budget 190 words normalized and 180 on the w10 canvas, advisory floor 130 and 160, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- Every phase repeats one anatomy in the same order, so the eye compares phases row by row.
- A milestone rail or track runs across the top of the phases with one node aligned over each phase.
- A band spanning all phases sits beneath the phase body and carries what persists across every phase.
- Arrow-shaped phase headers, chevrons or banner arrows, carry the time order without numbering.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A folio, wordmark, confidentiality line, footnote or caveat banner sits at the foot on 5 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The span band becomes the lowest zone, and any caveat folds into the phase it qualifies or moves to the notes. |
| glyphs | Curly apostrophes, arrow glyphs or en dashes in durations on 3 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash, arrow glyph or smart quote in text. | Durations read as a span with the word to, so the duration line needs a wider slot under each header. |
| 8pt-floor | Mini-diagram labels, rail legends or the page number run below the floor on 2 top exemplars. | Every run at 8 pt or above. | The rail legend goes and mini-diagram labels shrink to one word each, so each phase column keeps its height. |
| word-budget | Capability cells and flow cards run to paragraphs on 2 top exemplars. | A per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Each cell drops to one line and each phase flow to a few boxes, with detail moved to the notes. |
| no-subtitle | A lead line sits tight under the title on 1 top exemplar. | No subtitle under the title, which is one claim of 14 words or fewer. | The lead line is cut and the phase headers move up. |
| first-person | The title or phase questions speak as we or our on 1 top exemplar. | No first or second person. | Phase questions become third-person decision questions. |
| left-accent | A gradient edge bar runs down the slide edge on 1 top exemplar. | An accent bar only where it encodes a state that can change. | The edge bar goes, and the current phase is marked by its rail node instead. |
| flat-fill | A banner or foot band carries a vertical gradient on 1 top exemplar. | Flat fills, no gradients. | Any surviving band takes one flat tint. |
| other | Changes outside the named rules appear on 3 top exemplars, among them sentence titles past the word limit and card roles keyed by color alone. | The title is one claim of 14 words or fewer, and identity is never carried by color alone. | Titles are cut to one claim, and card roles carry a label or glyph beside their tone. |
