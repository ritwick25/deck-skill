# Governance model

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a22 | f04 | hierarchy | v1 |

## Use when
- A joint engagement must show its forums, how often each meets, who attends from both parties and what each forum decides.
- The audience needs to see how an issue escalates, to whom and how fast, before it signs off on the operating rhythm.

## Avoid when
- The slide introduces named people with portraits; use team-roster instead.
- Duties are assigned to owners phase by phase in a grid; use coverage-matrix instead.
- The tiers are platform layers rather than forums; use layered-architecture instead.

## Components
- Tier blocks, columns or pyramid levels, 3 (2 to 28) items in the dominant repeat, each naming one forum and its cadence.
- Connectors 1 (0 to 6) per slide, each leaving a tier and landing on the row, role list or counterpart it keys to.
- Icons 8 (0 to 37) per slide, role glyphs or forum glyphs in one family, never portraits or party logos.

## Density
Content words 138 (117 to 251) across 5 top exemplars, with 30 (17 to 92) text shapes per slide.

## Word budget
Budget 180 words normalized and 130 on the w10 canvas, advisory floor 120, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- Tiers named strategic, tactical and operational read top to bottom, and each tier keys across to one row of a matrix whose columns are the checkpoint and each party's attendees.
- Client and vendor sides mirror each other across a center column of forums or tier labels, so every forum shows a counterpart on both sides.
- Escalation reads as numbered steps, each naming who intervenes and the time or status that triggers it, set in a column beside the cadence device.
- Each cadence or forum column sits under a filled header tab carrying its name, with ownership and decision rows repeated in the same order under every tab.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A confidentiality line, wordmark, folio, tagline or foot band appears on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The tagline and summary bands go to the speaker notes, and the tiers and matrix take the height. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar, a title edge bar or a tapering wedge decorates the slide on 3 top exemplars. | A colored left or top accent bar only where it encodes a changeable state. | Remove the bars and the wedge, and let the tier headers carry the hierarchy by position and order. |
| no-subtitle | A sentence line sits under the title or under section heads on 2 top exemplars. | No subtitle under the title. | Fold the point into the one-claim title, and the cadence device moves up. |
| flat-fill | A gradient edge bar and foot band appear on 2 top exemplars. | Flat fills, no gradients. | Remove the gradient bars rather than flattening them, since they carry no content. |
| glyphs | Arrow glyphs in escalation routes, en dashes or curly apostrophes appear on 2 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash or arrow glyph in text. | Write escalation routes with the word to, or draw them as connectors between role chips. |
| word-budget | Agenda lists and summary bands push content words to or over the budget on 2 top exemplars. | Per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Cut each agenda list to its two strongest items so every row holds the same count. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows lift team cards and the role pool on 1 top exemplar. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Keep the hairline outlines and drop the effects. |
| first-person | The title speaks for the vendor in the first person on 1 top exemplar. | No first or second person. | Restate the title as a third-person claim about the governance cadence. |
| other | Party logos in headers, portraits with names, tier tints and status hues outside the brand set appear on 5 top exemplars. | Role labels for each party, role glyphs in place of portraits, one neutral ground with hairline separators, accents from the brand set only, and a title of one claim in at most 14 words. | Column headers become text labels and status meaning rides on a mark plus label, never on hue alone. |
