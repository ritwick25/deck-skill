# Case study

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a26 | f05 | claim-proof | v1 |

## Use when
- One delivered engagement is the proof, and the audience must read its context and need, what was built and the reported outcomes on one page.
- The build is small enough to draw as one flow, one set of lanes or one stack, and the outcomes are reported figures that close the page.

## Avoid when
- Several cases share the page as proof of breadth; use case-wall instead.
- The use case is proposed and not yet delivered; use deep-dive instead.
- Only the figures matter and the build is not shown; use headline-figures instead.
- The slide lists clients, awards or experience rather than one engagement; use credentials instead.

## Components
- Columns or panels, 2 (2 to 3) in the dominant repeat, typically context, build and outcomes in that reading order.
- Connectors 0 (0 to 1) per slide, most cases carry none, and the lane variant wires inputs through agents to outputs.
- Icons 1 (1 to 16) per slide, step discs in the build flow and a few context glyphs in one line family.

## Density
Content words 170 (112 to 238) across 5 top exemplars, with 18 (5 to 43) text shapes per slide.

## Word budget
Budget 190 words normalized and 190 on the w10 canvas, advisory floor 160, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- The page reads left to right as context, then build, then outcomes, each column under its own header, so the case is told in a fixed order.
- A short context element names the need before the build begins: a need card, a background column, a problem panel or a case description.
- The build is drawn as one small flow: icon discs joined by chevrons, a pipeline of steps, or rows that run from a named use case through its questions to an outcome panel.
- The outcomes close the page as large figures with short labels, either white figure cards on a solid rail at the right edge or a strip of figure tiles.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A confidentiality line, master wordmark, gradient foot band, page number or logo lockup appears on 3 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The outcome rail runs to the bottom margin and the columns take the freed height. |
| word-budget | Step descriptions, background bullets and scope strips push content words over budget on 4 top exemplars. | Per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Each step keeps one line, the context keeps three short lines and the scope strips merge into the build frame. |
| other | Vendor and product names in titles and headers, third-party logos, a decorative edge bar and two-line titles appear on 5 top exemplars. | A title of one claim in at most 14 words, neutral headers, and text labels or one neutral stack row in place of logos. | Headers become neutral labels such as context, what was built and reported outcomes, and the stack reads as plain labels. |
| 8pt-floor | Screenshot text, a pasted pipeline image and body lines render at or below the floor on 4 top exemplars. | Type at or above 8 pt everywhere. | Redraw the build natively with labels at the floor or above, or replace a stepped set of screens with one cropped screen. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar decorates the slide on 1 top exemplar. | A colored left or top accent bar only where it encodes a changeable state. | Remove the bar, and the context column starts at the margin. |
| no-subtitle | A case label line with the client sector sits under the title on 2 top exemplars. | No subtitle under the title. | Carry the sector in the context column instead. |
| glyphs | Before and after figures use an arrow glyph and value ranges use an en dash on 2 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash or arrow glyph in text. | Write figure ranges with the word to, or draw a small arrow shape between the two values. |
| flat-fill | A dark gradient strip over the industry photo appears on 1 top exemplar. | Flat fills, no gradients. | Drop the strip, or drop the photo and set the industry as a plain label. |
| shadow | A soft shadow lifts the solution card on 1 top exemplar. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Keep a hairline outline and drop the effect. |
