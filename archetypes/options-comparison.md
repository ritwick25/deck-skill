# Options comparison

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a24 | f05 | compare | v1 |

## Use when
- Two or more alternatives must be held to the same criteria or the same axis, and the audience must see which one is recommended and why.
- A decision is being asked for, so each option carries a score, a count or a finding and one option is marked as the choice.

## Avoid when
- The slide shows one subject today and in its target form; use from-to-shift instead.
- Many candidates are ranked on two axes such as value and effort; use prioritization-matrix instead.
- The slide sets what exists against what is missing; use gap-analysis instead.
- The options are priced packages for the buyer to choose among; use commercials instead.

## Components
- Option items, 5 (2 to 6) in the dominant repeat, whether option rows, scored cells, paired panels or overlapping circles.
- Connectors 1 (0 to 60) per slide, most slides carry almost none, and the dense cases draw a mesh against a shared block as the argument itself.
- Icons 3 (0 to 4) per slide, a persona glyph, tick marks or platform glyphs, never decoration on every option.

## Density
Content words 98 (79 to 291) across 5 top exemplars, with 34 (12 to 53) text shapes per slide.

## Word budget
Budget 170 words normalized and 90 on the w10 canvas, advisory floor 80, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- Each option sits under its own named header bar, pill or header card, so the alternatives read as parallel columns or panels before any cell is read.
- The criteria run as fixed rows read across every option, so each cell compares directly with the cell beside it.
- The chosen option is marked by a solid fill or a heavier outline against an outlined or plain alternative, with a label that names it as the choice.
- Each option carries one figure, a score box, a cell value or a large count, so the ranking reads from the numbers before the prose.
- A verdict band or verdict card names the winner and gives a one or two line reason.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A confidentiality line, client wordmark, page number, owner strip or gradient foot band appears on 5 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The verdict lives in the title and the verdict band, and the options take the freed height. |
| other | Titles name a step or a topic instead of a claim, kickers sit above them, party logos sit inside the options and a second accent hue appears on 5 top exemplars. | A title of one claim in at most 14 words, no kicker, role labels in place of logos, and accents from the brand set only. | The title states which option wins, and the chosen option is marked by outline weight and a label rather than by a second hue. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar, a short rule under the title or full-height bars on triad columns decorate the slide on 4 top exemplars. | A colored left or top accent bar only where it encodes a changeable state. | Remove the bars, and the outline on the chosen option becomes the only mark that encodes state. |
| first-person | The title or a column heading speaks for the vendor in the first person on 3 top exemplars. | No first or second person. | Restate the title as a third-person claim and rename headings to neutral nouns. |
| flat-fill | Gradient edge bars, foot bars or pill headers appear on 3 top exemplars. | Flat fills, no gradients. | Option headers take one flat fill, and the edge and foot bars are removed rather than flattened. |
| no-subtitle | A stage pill or an italic line sits under the title on 2 top exemplars. | No subtitle under the title. | Drop the line and fold any needed context into the context zone beside the options. |
| glyphs | The title trails into an ellipsis glyph on 1 top exemplar. | No em dash, en dash, arrow glyph or ellipsis in text. | End the title on a full claim. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows lift the option panels on 1 top exemplar. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Keep the hairline outlines and drop the effects. |
| word-budget | A problem, why and how row on top of the scored grid pushes content words over the ceiling on 1 top exemplar. | Per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Drop the context column that restates the title, and let the scored grid take the height. |
