# Problem framing

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a05 | f02 | claim-proof | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience must accept the problem before any answer is shown, and the problem breaks into a few parallel pressures, causes or sectors that each deserve a headed column.
- Separate causes land on one shared system or one shared consequence, and the slide must make that convergence visible with a band or a shared field under the columns.
- The story runs from today through a mandate to a required end state, and each of those stages needs its own device rather than a uniform grid.

## Avoid when
- The point is the current process walked stage by stage, use as-is-process (a14) instead.
- The point is what exists set against what is missing, use gap-analysis (a25) instead.
- The pressures are really reasons to buy from the vendor, use why-us (a07) instead.
- The columns state how the work will be done rather than why it is needed, use principles (a06) instead.

## Components
- Headed columns or cards in a repeat of 4 (2 to 8), each card 45% (22 to 47) of the slide width.
- Icons per slide 0 (0 to 10), used as column markers or pins on the shared field, never as decoration on every row.
- Connectors per slide 0 (0 to 11), present only where columns drop onto a shared field.
- Numbered discs or large numerals index the pressures, set at 11 pt (8 to 40).

## Density
Text shapes per slide 29 (19 to 35). The column zone fills 57% (23 to 63) of the slide height and 94% (50 to 96) of its width, so the columns are the governing visual and the band below is a rail, not a second body.

## Word budget
Word budget 250, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 207 (163 to 361), so most references must lose copy to fit.

## Device notes
- Every column or card opens with a solid header bar carrying a short caps label, so the set reads as peers before any body is read (d01-s063, d01-s064, d07-s002).
- Numbered discs or large numerals index the pressures, which lets a presenter call them by number (d01-s063, d21-s003, d07-s002, d03-s007).
- A full-width band sits under the columns at 78% (77 to 79) of the slide height and gathers the consequence, the benchmark figures or the shared pattern (d01-s063, d01-s064, d07-s002, d03-s007).
- One panel set apart on the right closes the read with the required end state or the gap figures, so the pressures resolve into one stated need (d03-s007, d21-s003).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| light-bg | Dark stat bands, a dark takeaway band, dark end-state and figure panels, and a faded photo behind the map, on 4 top exemplars. | The band becomes a light row with a hairline above each label, and the end-state panel becomes a flat light or brand-tint surface with navy text. | The band no longer anchors the slide by weight, so its figures must carry it by size and the band keeps its full width. |
| flat-fill | Gradient edge and foot strips and a gradient dependencies band, on 3 top exemplars. | Flat fills only, the dependencies band becomes a flat brand tint. | No layout change beyond deleting the edge and foot strips, which returns their height to the columns. |
| shadow | Soft blurred shadows under the numbered markers, on 1 top exemplar. | Hairlines plus at most a 1 px shadow. | Markers read flat, so their fill and numeral carry the index. |
| 8pt-floor | Source lines and captions set at 7.5 pt (7 to 8), on 3 top exemplars. | Every run at or above 8 pt, with each source cut to the publisher name. | Shorter source lines fit the larger size inside the band without adding a row. |
| no-footer | Confidentiality lines, wordmarks, page numbers and foot strips, on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number or confidentiality line, and the takeaway band moves into the title claim when it only restates it. | The columns extend down into the freed margin. |
| no-subtitle | A lead line under the title, on 1 top exemplar. | Fold the lead line into the title claim. | The columns start higher, directly under the title zone. |
| glyphs | Em dashes, en dash ranges and arrow chains in row text, on 3 top exemplars. | Commas, colons and the word to. | Rows grow by a few characters, so trim each row to one line. |
| first-person | A first-person possessive in the title, on 2 top exemplars. | No first or second person, the title states the problem as a claim. | None on geometry. |
| word-budget | Content words run 207 (163 to 361) across the top exemplars. | Hold the archetype budget of 250, never above 250. | Each column item becomes a bold label plus one line, and columns end at their content or share one row count. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar that encodes no state, on 2 top exemplars. | No accent bar unless it encodes a state that can change. | The title starts at the shared left margin. |
| other | Titles run 15 (5 to 27) words, some as a keyword plus a second claim line, with a mono kicker above. | One claim of 14 words or fewer, up to 3 keywords in the brand color, no kicker. | The title zone holds one or two lines, and the column zone gains the height. |

## Variants
- Twin numbered ladders over a figure band, causes against why it is hard (d01-s063).
- A numbered difficulty list beside one panel stacking a before range over an after range (d21-s003).
- Factor columns dropping onto a shared map over a consequence strip (d07-s002).
- Today, mandate and end state as headed zones over a dependencies band (d03-s007).
- A square grid of sector cards that repeats one situation and response recipe (d01-s064).
