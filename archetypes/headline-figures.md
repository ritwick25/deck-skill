# Headline figures

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a41 | f09 | kpi-band | v1 |

## Use when
- A few large figures are the argument, a finding, a result or the size of the prize, and each needs its own explanation, driver or source beneath it.
- A value equation or headline range must be broken into terms or levers so each lever reads with its own evidence.

## Avoid when
- The figures describe the firm's own scale, reach or recognition rather than the finding, so use credentials instead.
- The figure is the price of the work and what it buys, so use commercials instead.

## Components
- Dominant repeat of tiles or cards, 4 (3 to 4) items at 23% (20 to 43) of the canvas width, with a gutter of 1% (0 to 1).
- Icons 4 (0 to 15) per slide, heading the figures, the equation terms or the driver cards.
- Connectors 0 (0 to 1) per slide, so figures and their drivers are tied by position rather than by arrows.
- One figure row of large numerals, ranges or equation terms, each with a short label, above a row of explaining cards.

## Density
Content words 164 (68 to 270) across 5 exemplars, title words 9 (5 to 15).

## Word budget
Budget 240 words normalized, 250 on the w13 canvas and 140 on the w10 canvas, advisory floor 110, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- The figures sit in one row of equal tiles or columns across the content width, each a large numeral or range over a short label.
- A second row of cards beneath the figures explains, decomposes or sources them, and its count need not match the figure count.
- Icons head either the figures or the explaining cards, one per item, never both rows at once.
- Explaining cards close on a cited source or one evidence stat, so each claim carries its own proof.
- A full-width band names the period or the set of figures before the row it governs.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | A confidentiality line, page number, logo lockup or foot takeaway closes the slide on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The foot sentence folds into the title or the notes, and the source list moves to the notes or an appendix page. |
| word-budget | Finding panels, cited driver cards, a metrics band and a phase strip push words past the budget on 3 top exemplars. | A per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Driver cards keep a lead-in and one line, citations go to the notes, and secondary bands leave the slide. |
| first-person | The title, a subtitle or a band label speaks as we, our or you on 2 top exemplars. | No first or second person. | Titles and band labels are recast in the third person as a claim about the client's own systems. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows sit under roundels or outlined pain cards on 2 top exemplars. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Cards and roundels take a hairline edge only. |
| no-subtitle | An italic line or a period bar acts as a subtitle under the title on 2 top exemplars. | No subtitle under the title, which is one claim of 14 words or fewer. | The period or the basis folds into the title claim and the figure row moves up. |
| left-accent | A short title edge bar or thick foot bars under driver cards mark no state on 2 top exemplars. | An accent bar only where it encodes a state that can change. | The bars go, and a hairline above each card label groups the figures as a data sheet. |
| glyphs | Em dashes in stat captions and panel bodies on 1 top exemplar. | No em dash, en dash, arrow glyph or smart quote in text. | Captions break on commas or full stops, so each caption needs room for a slightly longer line. |
| light-bg | A photo band under a dark overlay carries the stat row on 1 top exemplar. | Light slide ground. The photo goes and the stat row sits on a solid near-black (ink-deep) band with paper labels. | The band keeps its weight, numerals take accent-bright on the dark fill, and the roundels keep the brand disc. |
| other | Changes outside the named rules appear on 5 top exemplars, among them kickers above the title, titles of up to 15 words, raster hub pictures and equal rounded tile walls. | The title is one claim of 14 words or fewer, every label is live text and figures read as a data sheet. | The kicker goes, the hub is redrawn natively, and tile fills give way to a hairline above each label. |
