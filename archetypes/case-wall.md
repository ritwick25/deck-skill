# Case wall

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a11 | f02 | catalogue | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience needs to see the breadth of delivered proof at once, several engagements each reduced to a headline and one outcome figure.
- Every case can be read against the same fields, a sector line, what was needed, what was delivered and what it changed, so the wall scans across as well as down.
- The cases share a theme or a set of sectors that can sit as a band under them and link onward to the full stories.

## Avoid when
- One engagement needs to be told in full, use case-study (a26) instead.
- The cases converge on one shared implication, use convergence-hub (a36) instead.
- The proof is firm scale, reach and recognition rather than engagements, use credentials (a43) instead.
- Three to six large figures are the whole argument with no case behind each, use headline-figures (a41) instead.

## Components
- Icons per slide 5 (0 to 9), one sector or case icon per case, never one per line.
- Pictures per slide 5 (0 to 9), icon tiles or small photos, one per case at most.
- Case columns separated by a gap of 4% (3 to 5) of the slide width, or by a dashed gray vertical in place of a gap.
- Connectors per slide 0 (0 to 4), only where a spine or numbered tags tie the cases to something shared.

## Density
Text shapes per slide 31 (17 to 35). The case zone fills 67% (53 to 78) of the slide height, and the outcome zone starts at 45% (36 to 72) from the top.

## Word budget
Word budget 190, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 177 (164 to 258).

## Device notes
- Each case lands its outcome as one isolated figure in the brand color beside a short outcome phrase, in a stat card, a pill, a figure grid or a ledger row (d16-s014, d03-s011, d07-s011, d01-s046).
- A short caps sector line describes each client by sector and scale, never by name, above a bold case headline (d16-s014, d07-s011, d01-s046, d09-s056).
- Cases sit as peer columns split by dashed gray verticals rather than boxed one by one (d16-s014, d09-s056, d01-s046).
- One icon tile, sector icon or spine disc marks each case before its text (d09-s056, d07-s011, d01-s046).
- Panels or a band under the cases name the sector or theme each case serves, at 67% (65 to 69) of the slide height (d09-s056, d07-s011).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8pt-floor | Sector lines, tags, pill phrases and paragraphs set below the floor, on 2 top exemplars. | Sector lines cut to two or three words and paragraphs to one line, all at the floor or above | Fewer words per cell, so the figures carry the argument. |
| first-person | A possessive title opener, on 2 top exemplars. | One claim in the third person naming the vendor by role | None on geometry. |
| flat-fill | Gradient top rules on stat cards and a gradient foot band, on 2 top exemplars. | Flat hairlines | None on geometry. |
| glyphs | En dashes in figure ranges and bullet characters in cells, on 3 top exemplars. | The word to in ranges and hairline-split lines in place of bullets | None on geometry. |
| left-accent | A colored top rule on every stat card and a gradient left edge bar, on 2 top exemplars. | A hairline above the figure, and no edge bar since neither encodes a state | Cells and title start at the shared left margin. |
| light-bg | A dark rounded theme band under the cards, on 1 top exemplar. | A light gray band with a hairline, the numbered tiles kept | None on geometry. |
| no-footer | Confidentiality lines, page numbers, logo lockups and a foot takeaway strip, on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot strip, and takeaways move to the notes | The foot band or the outcome row sits on the bottom margin. |
| no-subtitle | A sentence under the title restating the case count, on 1 top exemplar. | The count folds into the title claim | The case headers move up into the freed band. |
| other | Tool names and the vendor named in cells, photos carrying the cards, a navigation roundel and link pills, on 5 top exemplars. | Capability categories and role words, one sector icon per case, plain text links | Photos give way to icons, freeing height for the outcome row. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows on stat cards, pills and theme tiles, on 2 top exemplars. | Hairline borders with at most a 1 px shadow | None on geometry. |
| word-budget | Paragraph copy that brings the wall to the ceiling, on 1 top exemplar. | Paragraphs trimmed to one line per case | The outcome figures gain room and read first. |
