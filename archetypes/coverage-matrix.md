# Coverage matrix

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a31 | f06 | matrix | v1 |

## Use when
- Two lists cross, such as owners by duties, phases by domains or stages by terms, and every cell must state or mark what applies where.
- The audience must see at a glance which combinations are covered, shared or empty, and read along a row or down a column.
- One shared control or discipline runs across every column and belongs in the same picture as the cells.

## Avoid when
- Each row is a record of text read one row at a time against the same columns: use a33 reference-table.
- Items are plotted or scored on value and effort to decide which go first: use a32 prioritization-matrix.
- Use cases are placed on the value chain with no per cell statement: use a30 use-case-map.

## Components
- A header row naming the columns once, as solid bars, header cells or numbered stage discs.
- A rail of row labels down the left edge, as solid tabs or blocks, or a label cell leading each row card.
- The cell grid: short text cells, filled or empty phase cells, or shaded cells with no text, one per row and column.
- Optional band spanning the full grid width for the control, benchmark or discipline shared by every column.
- Optional legend of swatches decoding the cell shades or marks.
- Icons per slide 0 (0 to 5), text shapes per slide 29 (23 to 100).

## Density
Content words 165 (109 to 417) over 5 exemplars. Title words 10 (7 to 16). Cell grid width 93% (82 to 95) and height 58% (34 to 79) of the canvas.

## Word budget
Word budget 250 pooled, held at the 250 ceiling because the densest exemplars run past it, 250 on the standard canvas and 240 on the wide canvas. Advisory floor 150.

## Device notes
- Each column is named once in a header row and its tint or hue is carried down into its cells, so a cell reads its column without a repeated label.
- Rows are named by a solid tab or block on the left rail, darker than the cells, so the eye reads across a row from its label.
- Cell fill carries the answer where text would crowd: stepped shades of one hue, or filled against empty phase cells, decoded by a short key.
- A concern shared by every column sits in one band spanning the full grid width, under the cells, rather than repeated in each cell.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-footer | Confidentiality lines, folios, wordmarks, gradient foot bands and a dark foot summary band, 4 flagged in the top set. | No foot chrome. A shared control becomes a spanning row of the grid, not a foot band. | The spanning band moves up to sit directly under the last row of cells. |
| no-subtitle | A sentence line under the title and an axis strip beside it, 3 flagged. | Delete the subtitle, the header row carries the frame. | The header row and grid move up into the freed band. |
| light-bg | A black title band, near black row blocks and a dark discipline band, 2 flagged. | White ground. Row headers stay solid near-black (ink-strong) blocks with paper text, the title stands on the ground with no band, and a shared discipline band stays a solid near-black or brand fill. | Row labels keep their width and their weight, so the eye still reads across a row from its dark block. |
| word-budget | Cells holding method lines, capability lists and a measure column, far over the ceiling, 2 flagged. | A header plus one line per cell within the word budget, detail moved to notes. | Cells shorten, so row height drops and the grid can carry more rows. |
| 8pt-floor | Cell text near half the floor, italic member lists and legend definitions under it, 2 flagged. | Every run at 8 pt or larger. | Widen the row label column or cut cell text to fit at the floor. |
| left-accent | Lane hue left bars encoding fixed identity and a gradient left edge bar, 2 flagged. | Remove the bars, the filled cells carry the lane. | Row cards run clean to the margin. |
| flat-fill | Gradient foot bands and edge bars, 2 flagged. | Flat brand tint, or remove the bar. | None on layout. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows under header bars and row tabs, 1 flagged. | Flat fills with a hairline, at most a 1 px shadow. | No change to geometry. |
| glyphs | Leading and trailing ellipses in a title, a dash in a title, arrow chains in cells and legend, 2 flagged. | Colons, the word to, or a full stop. | None on layout. |
| first-person | Column captions and cell lines written as we decide, we build and a possessive, 2 flagged. | Role nouns and plain noun captions such as decisions, builds and proof. | Captions shorten to one or two words. |
| emoji | Emoji in the column headers, 1 flagged. | Line icons, or no icon. | None on layout. |
| other | Fragment titles continuing a prior slide, titles past the word limit, a mono kicker, product names in cells, a fully saturated high shade, 4 flagged. | One standalone claim of 14 words or fewer, generic category nouns, the high shade stepped to the brand blue so labels keep contrast. | The title band shortens to one or two lines and the darkest cells keep legible ink. |
