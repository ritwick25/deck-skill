# Scope and assumptions

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a29 | f05 | compare | v1 |

## Use when
- A proposal must fence its commitment: what is in, what is out, and the assumptions and dependencies it rests on, so nothing is promised by omission.
- A reader will test delivery against a list later, so each item must be short, numbered and led by a bold term that can be checked.

## Avoid when
- The page sets price and terms, so use commercials instead.
- The page weighs alternative approaches against each other, so use options-comparison instead.
- The page must also carry the ask, the answer and the plan on one page, so use executive-summary instead.
- The page explains one use case or mechanism in depth, so use deep-dive instead.

## Components
- List panels, 2 (2 to 6) items in the dominant repeat, each a column of numbered or bulleted items under a named header.
- Connectors 0 (0 to 0) per slide, so the lists carry the page and lines stay out of it.
- Icons 0 (0 to 1) per slide, used as item marks or as the one glyph that flags the exclusion block.
- Numbered items, each a bold lead-in term followed by one clause.

## Density
Content words 281 (3 to 353) across 5 top exemplars, with 6 (1 to 27) text shapes per slide. Text-led, so the word budget is the binding constraint.

## Word budget
Budget 250 words normalized and 250 on the w10 canvas, held at the ceiling because the top exemplars run long, advisory floor 150, under the ceiling of 250.

## Device notes
- Paired columns sit under filled tab headers of equal width, in scope beside a second in-scope column, or assumptions beside dependencies.
- Each list item opens with a number and a bold lead-in term, then one clause, so a reader can check the item off later.
- What is out is set against what is in by its own mark, a darker narrowing beam or a block headed by a distinct glyph, never by position alone.
- Assumptions and dependencies are kept as separate lists side by side rather than merged into one.

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| word-budget | Long functional lists and multi-clause items push content words to or over the budget on 4 top exemplars. | Per-archetype word budget under the ceiling of 250. | Each item keeps its bold term and one short clause, and the long detail moves to the speaker notes or an appendix table. |
| left-accent | A full-height rail beside each list panel or an edge bar beside the title decorates the slide on 4 top exemplars. | A colored left or top accent bar only where it encodes a changeable state. | A hairline above each list heading replaces the rails, and the title loses its edge bar. |
| no-footer | A confidentiality line, its hairline, a vendor mark or a page number sits at the foot on 4 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band. | The lists run to the bottom margin with nothing under them. |
| 8pt-floor | List bodies run under the floor on 3 top exemplars. | No run under 8 pt. | Lists grow to the floor, so each item is cut to one line and the longest list moves to a numbered table. |
| glyphs | En dashes in ranges, smart apostrophes or an ellipsis appear on 2 top exemplars. | No em dash, en dash or arrow glyph in text. | Ranges read with the word to, quotes are straight and the ellipsis goes. |
| first-person | The title speaks for the vendor in the first person on 1 top exemplar. | No first or second person. | The title states a third-person claim about what is fixed before build. |
| no-subtitle | A gray lead-in strap sits under the title on 1 top exemplar. | No subtitle under the title. | The strap goes and the scope beam carries the lens idea alone. |
| shadow | A soft shadow lifts the list panels on 1 top exemplar. | Hairlines with at most a 1 px shadow. | Panels keep their hairline outline only. |
| other | Topic-label titles with a section prefix or brackets, an empty body, a duplicated list, red open-item runs or off-palette header tabs appear on 5 top exemplars. | A title of one claim in at most 14 words, accents from the brand set only, and status carried by a mark plus label. | Titles state what is committed, open items move to one marked status column, and a page with no lists is filled or deleted. |
