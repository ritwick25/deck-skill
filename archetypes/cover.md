# Cover

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a01 | f01 | title | v1 |

## Use when
- The deck opens and has to name the engagement, the parties and the date in one title stack before any argument starts.
- A working session opens with the people in the room, so a row of portrait discs with name and role replaces the hero visual.

## Avoid when
- The page marks a part boundary inside the deck, so use section-divider (a03) instead.
- The page signs off with contact detail or a last claim, so use closing (a04) instead.
- The page has to list the parts of the session in order, so use agenda (a02) instead.

## Components
- One flush-left title stack: an optional kicker, the title, one label line and the date, all on one left edge.
- A logo lockup of the party marks, split by a thin vertical rule, set above the title stack or at top right.
- One visual that balances the stack on the right: a vector illustration of the subject, a flat brand shape, a native diagram, or a small corner motif of joined lines and discs.
- Optional attendee row: portrait discs in an even row, each over a bold name and a short role line, split by hairlines.
- Content pictures per slide 3 (0 to 6), text shapes 4 (3 to 7).

## Type roles
| Role | Size |
|---|---|
| title | 35 pt (28 to 44) |
| kicker | 11 pt (10 to 11) |
| label | 16 pt (12.5 to 18.75) |
| caption | 11 pt (10 to 14) |

## Density
Sparse by design. Content words 26 (9 to 50) per slide, title words 6 (4 to 8). The visual carries the page and the stack stays one block.

## Word budget
Word budget 40 with an advisory floor of 10, under the hard ceiling of 250. The title stays within 14 words.

## Device notes
- The title stack sits flush left against one large visual that fills the right side of the canvas (d18-s001, d13-s001, d17-s001).
- A tall brand bar runs down the left edge of the title stack and encodes no state (d18-s001, d13-s001).
- The party marks pair up in one lockup split by a thin rule (d13-s001, d01-s001).
- The date closes the stack as its last line, set small under the title or the byline (d18-s001, d13-s001, d17-s001, d21-s001).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| flat-fill | Gradient waves, washes and panels behind the visual, on 3 top exemplars. | One flat brand-tint shape of the same silhouette, or the plain light ground. | The right side reads as a single flat field, so the visual must carry content rather than mood. |
| left-accent | A tall brand bar binds the title stack on 2 top exemplars. | No bar. Shared left alignment alone groups kicker, title, label and date. | The stack needs a strict type ladder and even spacing, since no rule holds it together. |
| light-bg | Raster photo fields behind or beside the stack, or a dark navy ground under the whole cover, on 2 top exemplars. | Light slide ground, with any visual held to the right side and drawn as vector. A brand or near-black block inside the cover stays a solid fill, its text in that fill's ON_FILL role. | The title zone keeps full contrast and the visual zone shrinks toward the right side. Pastel only as a full-bleed ground, never as a panel. |
| no-subtitle | An italic promise or proposal line under the title on 4 top exemplars. | The promise folds into the title as its one claim, or it goes. | The title carries the claim, so the stack loses a line and the date moves up. |
| no-footer | A web address line under the date on 2 top exemplars. | No web line, no footer, no page number. | The stack ends on the date. |
| shadow | A soft shadow under the diagonal band on 1 top exemplar. | Hairlines plus at most a 1 px shadow. | Any band reads flat and sits behind the visual without lift. |
| glyphs | A dash joining the promise line to its named example, on 1 top exemplar. | A colon or a comma, or the promise line goes with the subtitle. | None on layout. |
| other | Raster product photos, generated illustrations and stock imagery carry the visual zone on 4 top exemplars. | A native vector diagram of the subject, a plain screen frame with a labelled grid, or empty ground. | The visual has to state the thesis, not decorate it, or the right side stays ground. |

## Variants
- Title stack beside hero visual, the default (d13-s001).
- Thesis cover, where the visual argues the deck's gap in one picture (d17-s001).
- Welcome cover, where an attendee row of portrait discs replaces the visual (d01-s001).
- Motif cover, where a small corner motif of joined lines and discs stands in for the hero visual (d21-s001).
