# Closing

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a04 | f01 | closing | v1 |

## Use when
- The deck ends and needs one last line: a sign-off with role-level contact detail beside a single attributed testimonial or the restated promise.
- The last frame should carry the argument once more, so the closing panel restates the deck's one-line promise.

## Avoid when
- The close asks for decisions with dated next steps, so use the-ask instead.
- The page marks a part boundary inside the deck, so use section-divider (a03) instead.
- The page opens the deck with the parties and the date, so use cover (a01) instead.

## Components
- A short sign-off line set large at the upper left, title words 2 (2 to 2), with a short rule under it.
- A contact block of role-level contact lines, an office list and the site, flush with the sign-off.
- A full-height flat panel on the right holding either one attributed testimonial under an oversized quote mark or the restated promise.
- Text shapes 8 (6 to 11) and content pictures 2 (0 to 2) per slide.

| Role | Size |
|---|---|

| body | 13.5 pt (11 to 16) |

| quote | 18.75 pt (18.75 to 18.75) |

## Density
Light. Content words 57 (32 to 57) per slide, split between the contact block and the panel. The panel holds one statement and no list.

## Word budget
Word budget 60 with an advisory floor of 40, under the hard ceiling of 250. The sign-off stays within 14 words.

## Device notes
- A full-height flat color panel takes the right side of the canvas and holds one statement (d16-s025, d18-s025, d12-s020).
- One attributed testimonial sits under an oversized quote mark inside the panel (d16-s025, d12-s020).
- A short rule sits under or above the closing line to mark it (d16-s025, d12-s020, d18-s025).
- The contact block lists offices by region under the sign-off, flush left (d16-s025, d18-s025, d12-s020).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| light-bg | A saturated full-height brand block with white text on 1 top exemplar. | A pale brand tint panel with ink text, so the ground stays light. | The panel separates by tint and edge, so its statement needs ink weight rather than reversed type. |
| left-accent | A tall edge bar beside the contact column on 1 top exemplar. | No bar. The column aligns to the margin. | The sign-off and contact lines group by alignment and spacing alone. |
| shadow | A blurred shadow on the panel edge on 1 top exemplar. | The flat fill boundary is the divider, with at most a 1 px shadow. | No layout change. |
| glyphs | A leading hyphen used as a dash before the attribution on 1 top exemplar. | The attribution sits on its own line with no dash. | The attribution needs its own type step to read as a source. |
| first-person | A possessive inside the testimonial on 1 top exemplar. | A verbatim attributed quotation is the one allowed carrier of the first person, so it stays quoted. | The quotation must be real and attributed by role, never written for the slide. |
| no-footer | A page number at the lower left on 1 top exemplar. | No page number or footer. | The contact block may run to the foot margin. |
| other | Personal email, named offices and a caps kicker that repeats the title on 2 top exemplars. | Role-level contact text and no kicker that restates the title. | The contact block gets shorter and the sign-off stands alone. |
