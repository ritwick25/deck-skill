# Input, process, output

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a15 | f03 | sequence | v1 |

## Use when
- The mechanism is best understood by what goes in and what comes out, with the method between them as numbered steps.
- The method is the argument, so it earns the widest panel and the inputs and outputs frame it as narrower columns.
- Each output or step can be proven by a small screen or evidence image, and reusable parts or further uses can sit in a strip below.

## Avoid when
- A long stage chain carries the story with no input and output framing, use process-flow (a13) instead.
- The point is which components sit inside a trust boundary and how they connect, use solution-architecture (a21) instead.
- Cases branch on rules into different actions, use decision-logic (a16) instead.
- One named record must be traced through every hop with real payloads, use worked-example (a18) instead.

## Components
- Panels or step cards in a repeat of 4 (2 to 6), each 13% (3 to 23) of the slide width and 9% (3 to 10) of its height.
- Connectors per slide 2 (0 to 7), a disc or block arrow on each gap between panels plus short links inside the step chain.
- Icons per slide 4 (0 to 43), one per listed input, step or output.
- Screens or insets per slide 0 (0 to 1), small proof thumbnails beside steps or inside the output panel.
- Optional cycle arrow or exception loop inside the method panel, and optional chips naming output formats or reusable parts.

## Density
Text shapes per slide 14 (11 to 56). The method panel is 43% (9 to 92) of the slide width, the widest of the three panels at the median, with the input and output widths in the zone table.

## Word budget
Word budget 250, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 124 (67 to 247).

## Device notes
- A pill or tab header names each panel and straddles or caps its top edge, so input, method and output read as one triptych (d02-s010, d10-s005, d13-s031).
- A disc or block arrow sits on each gap between panels to mark the handoff, never a long connector across the slide (d02-s010, d10-s005, d11-s003, d21-s005).
- The method panel carries numbered steps in discs or tiles, stacked or chained (d02-s010, d13-s031).
- Small proof thumbnails sit beside the steps or the outputs they show (d02-s010, d11-s003).
- Strips under the triptych name reusable parts, further use cases or the constraints the method holds to, at 53% (26 to 80) of the slide height (d02-s010, d13-s031).
- The method panel is the widest box on the row, so the mechanism reads as the subject and input and output as its edges (d13-s031, d21-s005).
