# As-is process

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a14 | f03 | sequence | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience must see where today's work breaks before any fix is shown, so the current process is walked stage by stage.
- Each pain can be pinned to the stage where it occurs, as a numbered marker, a struck return loop or a marked-up screen from today's tools.
- The cost of the current process can be sized beside the flow, so the cost of inaction reads next to its cause.

## Avoid when
- The stages show the proposed solution running end to end, use process-flow (a13) instead.
- Today and the target are drawn together on one slide, use from-to-shift (a23) instead.
- The pains stand alone with no process to pin them to, use problem-framing (a05) instead.
- Cases branch on rules into different actions, use decision-logic (a16) instead.

## Components
- Stage columns or cards in a repeat of 3 (3 to 5), each 18% (15 to 23) of the slide width and 9% (5 to 15) of its height.
- Connectors per slide 1 (0 to 9), chevrons between stage headers plus dashed leaders from a step to its evidence.
- Icons per slide 5 (0 to 21), one per stage heading, role or tool.
- Screens or insets per slide 0 (0 to 2), only where a pain is proven on a screen from today's tools.
- Optional numbered discs or a struck dashed return line marking each pain at its stage, and optional status states on capability cards with a short legend.

## Density
Text shapes per slide 36 (19 to 63). The stage columns fill 91% (34 to 95) of the slide width and 57% (40 to 68) of its height.

## Word budget
Word budget 250, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 214 (114 to 364), so the densest columns are cut to labels before they are borrowed.

## Device notes
- Chevrons or short arrows sit between stage headers or stage boxes, so the stages read left to right without a drawn spine (d15-s004, d01-s066, d13-s030, d01-s067).
- Each pain is pinned to its stage, by numbered discs under the stage boxes or by a dashed return line struck through with a cross (d01-s066, d15-s004).
- A side panel to the right of the flow sizes the cost of today's process with large numerals or a ring, at 69% (62 to 75) from the left edge (d10-s004, d01-s066).
- Screens or artwork from today's tools sit under the flow as proof, tied to the step they show (d10-s004, d01-s066).
- A row under the stages names each stage's output or the outcome per stakeholder, at 79% (73 to 85) of the slide height (d15-s004, d13-s030, d01-s067).
