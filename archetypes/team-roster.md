# Team roster

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a10 | f02 | catalogue | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience needs to meet the people who will do the work, by face, name and role, before or after the plan is presented.
- The team splits into named leads and a wider bench, so a portrait grid and compact grouped lists can share one page or a pair of pages.
- One or two leads deserve a page each, with a fixed profile frame that only changes its portrait, impact line and facts.

## Avoid when
- The page shows roles, forums and tiers with no people in them, use governance-model (a22) instead.
- The people are attendees welcomed on the opening page, use cover (a01) instead.
- The point is the firm's scale, reach and recognition rather than named individuals, use credentials (a43) instead.
- The proof is delivered engagements rather than the people, use case-wall (a11) instead.

## Components
- Pictures per slide 12 (2 to 31), almost all portraits cropped to a circle on roster pages and one square headshot on a profile page.
- Portrait columns separated by a gap of 8% (5 to 9) of the slide width.
- Icons per slide 1 (1 to 32), a single group glyph in the title disc, or one flag per roster row on the grouped variant.

## Density
Text shapes per slide 23 (5 to 30). The portrait grid fills 72% (45 to 86) of the slide width and 85% (57 to 86) of its height.

## Word budget
Word budget 250, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 121 (63 to 390).

## Device notes
- A large disc cropped by the left edge holds the group name under one glyph, and the portrait grid fills the space to its right (d01-s010, d01-s009).
- Every person is one circular portrait over a bold name and a short role line, never a sentence (d03-s002, d01-s010, d01-s009).
- Profile pages keep one frame fixed, a left rail of name block, square portrait and impact card beside a right biography under short caps section labels (d09-s052, d09-s053).
- Pale concentric rings radiate from the edge disc behind the grid, reprinted as flat tints or dropped (d01-s010, d01-s009).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| glyphs | En dashes after run-in heads and a smart apostrophe in a biography, on 1 top exemplar. | Colons after run-in heads and straight apostrophes | None on geometry. |
| left-accent | A gradient left edge bar running the slide height, on 2 top exemplars. | No accent bar, since it encodes no state | The edge disc sits directly on the left edge. |
| no-footer | Confidentiality lines, client wordmarks, page numbers and gradient foot bands, on 3 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot band | The last portrait row can drop to the bottom margin. |
| other | A thank-you headline, nationality flags, a label for a title and contact lines on a profile, on 5 top exemplars. | One claim about the team in the title, identity by name and role only, contact lines cut | The edge disc carries a short claim rather than a label, and the impact card closes the rail. |
| shadow | Blurred shadows under portrait circles, on 2 top exemplars. | Hairline outlines with at most a 1 px shadow | None on geometry. |
| word-budget | Full biographies in justified paragraphs on profile pages, on 2 top exemplars. | Each section cut to one or two lines of fact, the full biography moved to notes or an appendix | The biography column shortens and gains air between sections. |

## Variants
- Featured portrait grid beside grouped roster lists, split by a diagonal rule (d03-s002).
- Edge disc beside a full portrait grid for the wider bench (d01-s010).
- Edge disc beside a staggered grid for the core team (d01-s009).
- Profile page with bold run-in heads in the biography (d09-s052).
- Profile page with a lead paragraph over a plain list of recent work (d09-s053).
