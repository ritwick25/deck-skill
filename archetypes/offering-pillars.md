# Offering pillars

| Archetype | Family | Message shape | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| a08 | f02 | catalogue | v1 |

## Use when
- The audience needs to see what the offer is made of, a few peer parts, tiers or bands, each one something the client would buy.
- Every part delivers into one shared outcome, benefit set or control layer that can sit as a band under the parts.
- Each part can be proven by a miniature of what it produces, so the panel shows the output rather than describing it.

## Avoid when
- Every product or agent is listed with the same fields, use catalogue (a09) instead.
- The parts sit around a central core, use hub-and-spoke (a34) instead.
- The items are reasons to choose the vendor rather than parts to buy, use why-us (a07) instead.
- The parts are phases delivered over time, use phased-roadmap (a38) instead.

## Components
- Peer panels or tier cards in a repeat of 2 (2 to 4), each 24% (22 to 45) of the slide width and 25% (3 to 58) of its height.
- Icons per slide 8 (3 to 17), one per panel heading or one per listed item, never both.
- Connectors per slide 0 (0 to 1), mostly dashed rules splitting items inside a band, plus a spine where the parts run in order.
- Screens or insets per slide 0 (0 to 0), used only where a panel is proven by a miniature of its output.

## Density
Text shapes per slide 30 (18 to 56). The panel zone fills 48% (24 to 73) of the slide height and 93% (80 to 94) of its width.

## Word budget
Word budget 220, the upper quartile of the top exemplars capped at 250. Content words on the top exemplars run 142 (94 to 226).

## Device notes
- A full-width band under the panels gathers the shared outcome, benefits or controls, opened by a solid label block or an icon disc at its left end, at 76% (57 to 78) of the slide height (d03-s037, d01-s029, d01-s041).
- Panels stay peers: the same width, the same heading treatment and the same item count, so no part reads as the main one (d03-s037, d01-s029, d15-s003).
- Items inside a panel or band are split by dashed hairline rules rather than nested boxes (d01-s029, d01-s041, d01-s049).
- Each part carries one icon at its heading, and items below it are short bold leads with at most one line under each (d03-s037, d01-s041, d01-s049, d15-s003).

## Translation under the house rules
| Rule | Reference | House | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| first-person | A card intro that addresses the reader as your, on 1 top exemplar. | Neutral nouns for the buyer and the users. | None on geometry. |
| flat-fill | Multi-stop gradient edge and foot bars, on 2 top exemplars. | Removed rather than flattened. | The freed foot returns to the benefit band. |
| glyphs | En dashes and spaced hyphens between keyword and claim in titles, on 3 top exemplars. | A colon, or the keyword merged into the claim. | None on geometry. |
| left-accent | Gradient left edge bars and blue left bars on checklist rows, on 4 top exemplars. | No accent bar unless it encodes a state that can change, such as a reviewed row. | Title and panels start at the shared left margin. |
| no-footer | Confidentiality lines, wordmarks, page counters, foot summary lines and orange foot bands, on 5 top exemplars. | No footer, page number, confidentiality line or foot summary band, and a target outcome moves into the title. | The benefit band becomes the lowest element and sits on the bottom margin. |
| no-subtitle | A sentence under the title, on 3 top exemplars. | Fold it into the title claim. | Panels start directly under the title. |
| shadow | Blurred card shadows, on 1 top exemplar. | Hairline outlines plus at most a 1 px shadow. | None on geometry. |
| word-budget | Content words run 142 (94 to 226) across the top exemplars. | Hold the archetype budget of 220, never above 250. | Each item becomes a bold lead phrase plus at most one short line, or the inset carries the panel and the bullets go. |
| other | Label titles, mono kickers and eyebrows, a tier pill on every card, introduced accent hues per card, and a callout that restates the title, on 4 top exemplars, with titles of 9 (6 to 14) words. | One claim of 14 words or fewer with up to 3 keywords in the brand color, the deck sans for every label, order carried by position, and the house blue and gray for every card. | The callout is cut and the benefit band runs full width. |

## Variants
- Tier cards with checked capabilities over an outcome band (d03-s037).
- Twin numbered offer cards over a shared benefit strip (d01-s029).
- Output cards each proven by a miniature of their output (d15-s003).
- Roundels on a spine over capability boxes and a shared controls band (d01-s041).
- Why, what and value bands split into icon columns over an audience strip (d01-s049).
