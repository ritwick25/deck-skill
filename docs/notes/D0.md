## What changed
- Archetypes: DL11 serves gantt only, DL23 phased-roadmap only. Families moved onto the frozen taxonomy: DL13
  Axis of time or maturity, DL26 Directed sequence, DL31 Stacked layers.
- Content band (P03): every build ends on `finish()`, so every device sits inside x 0.50 to 12.90in. DL10, DL14,
  DL17, DL22 and DL24 were refitted by hand (no scaling); DL08 by redesign; the rest by `fit_band`.
  DL17's card row and DL28's road bleed on purpose and are named `bleed.*`. DL31 lost its whole-slide page frame.
- DL24 fans in: feeders 1-3 and 4-6 meet on two entry points of the hub through a trunk; one exit fans out to the
  five outcomes. Feeder samples shortened to fit the narrower cards.
- DL07: step descriptions inset 0.06in from both dashed dividers (box narrowed, zero side insets, 0.52in tall).
- DL08 redesigned: the 39-disc mesh and its 44 links are gone. Four named agents per domain column (status disc
  from the key, name 10.5pt, task 10pt), the spotlit column is `state.spotlight`, and one arrowed bus runs from its
  foot into every other domain. 0 of 67 runs at 9pt or below (was 22 of 49).
- DL14: labels raised to 9 to 10pt; target half pulled into the band; bent links redrawn on PowerPoint's route
  (see below); two links that crossed a label removed; flow labels knock their line out.
- DL16: bent links on PowerPoint's route; the Rollback label knocks its loop line out.
- Small tints (P16): every accent-tint or accent-wash shape under 4 sq in not named `state.*` is now card, in
  device.pptx and again at assembly. DL25's handoff discs reseated in their gaps.
- device.json gains `capacity_words`, `archetype_floors`, `short_of_floor`; samples and backgrounds re-synced.

## API (scripts/assemble_devices.py; a build imports it, scripts/ is already on its path)
- `finish(slide, theme) -> None`: `fit_band` then `remap_small_tints`. Call it last, before save.
- `fit_band(slide, lo=0.50, hi=12.90, *, title="slot.title") -> float`: maps every top-level shape's x from the
  device's own extent onto [lo, hi]; `bleed.*` stays; the title takes the title band; pictures, rotated shapes and
  small square shapes keep size and move by centre; tables scale grid columns. Returns the scale (1.0 = untouched).
  It narrows text boxes too, so a tight label can then fail P02: refit by hand when the scale is under about 0.99.
- `remap_small_tints(slide, theme, *, limit=4.0) -> list[str]`.
- `bent_points(el) -> list | None`: the route PowerPoint draws for a bentConnector2/3/4 (adjusts, flips, rotation).
- `bake_bent(slide, names=None) -> list[str]`: redraws bent connectors as unglued straight segments
  `<name>.part<k>` on that route, heads kept on the ends.
- `capacity(entry, floors()) -> dict`, `floors() -> {archetype: wide floor}`.
- CLI: `$PY scripts/assemble_devices.py [--no-render] [--out DIR]`. `--out` writes everything under DIR (dry run).
  It now refuses a family outside gates/families.json, records `paras` on each text slot, prints a fit or tint
  NOTE per device it had to correct, and writes a Words column and per-archetype floors into device-library.md.
