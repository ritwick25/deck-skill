# E3 arch sequence, DFD and C4 (kit/deckkit/arch_seq.py)

Built on arch.py's public notation only (FLOW_BY, BADGE_BY, BADGE_FILL, NAME_PT, ROLE_PT, LABEL_PT, CLEAR, LAST_LEG,
LABEL_INS). arch.py untouched. Example: `scripts/example_arch_seq.py` (4 slides, Quillmont canon). Tests:
`tests/test_arch_seq.py` (16, pass). Example gates: 26 of 27 pass; P26 fails only on the C4 orphan rule (request 1).

## Signatures
- `Sequence(*, head_w=None, mark=0.30)`; `.participant(id, mark, name, role=None)`; `.band(label, first, last)`
  (one trust band, label needs "name · fact"); `.message(src, dst, label, kind="call"|"reply"|"async") -> n`
  (src == dst is a self call); `.fragment(op, guard, first, last, *, else_at=None, else_guard=None)` (op alt, opt,
  loop, par, break, critical); `.render(slide, box, theme, name="seq", *, legend=True) -> metrics`.
- `DFD(grid=(cols, rows), *, weights=, row_weights=, level="context"|"1"|"2", threat=True)`;
  `.boundary(id, "Name · fact", at=(c, r, cs, rs), kind="trust"|"zone", parent=)`;
  `.process(id, "1", name, *, at, tech=, mark=)`; `.entity(id, name, role, *, at, mark=, out_of_scope=False)`;
  `.store(id, "D1", name, *, at, mark=)`; `.flow(src, dst, label, *, control=, route=, exit=, enter=, bend=, step=)`;
  `.render(slide, box, theme, name, *, legend=True, draft=False)`.
- `C4("context"|"container", grid=, weights=, row_weights=)`; `.person(id, name, desc, *, at)`;
  `.system(id, name, desc, *, at, span=, size=)`; `.external(id, name, desc, *, at, mark=)`;
  `.container(id, name, tech, desc, *, at, mark=)` (container view only); `.rel(src, dst, "intent · protocol", **kw)`;
  `.boundary(..., kind="system"|"enterprise")`; `.render(...)` as DFD.
- Grid positions may be fractional: `at=(1.5, 2)` centres a store under the gap between columns 1 and 2.
- `route` forces h, v, hv, vh, hvh or vhv; `exit`/`enter` set the port as a fraction of the side; `bend` the Z leg.
- `render(draft=True)` lists what does not fit in `metrics["problems"]` and draws the rest; never ship a draft.

## What it enforces
- Every message, flow and relationship labelled, at most 5 words; sequence messages numbered 1..N at the label
  start (no badges, spec 3.10); DFD flows numbered by 0.22in accent badges beside the source end, 1..N.
- Orthogonal routes edge to edge; no crossing, no run along another line or a boundary; else it raises.
- Labels, badges, chips measured and placed clear of every box, line and boundary edge (wholly in or out).
- Sequence: a call needs its reply unless inside break; alt operands share open calls; fragments nest or stand
  apart and lie wholly inside or outside the band; a band-edge gap widens until the crossing label fits one side.
- DFD: a process at one end of every flow; context level is 1 process, 0 stores; every boundary-crossing flow
  carries a marker on each intercept and one control chip, and a chip only on a crossing flow.
- C4: one system in focus on a context view; containers need technology and the system boundary.
- Boundaries hug their content (P08); labels state a fact after a middot (spec 1.6).

## Deliberate deviations from spec, house wins
- Process radius is the canvas 0.05in, not spec 3.8's 0.08in (P11).
- Crossing marker 0.12in, not 0.14in (an edge-centred 0.14in box breaks P05's 0.10in overlap rule).
- Async is dash with an open head (spec 1.4: a distinct dash per kind), not 3.10's solid open head.
- C4 technology is a "Container · tech" line beside a 0.28in mark, never a pill on every tile (CLAUDE.md 4).
- Example sequence has 6 participants, not Q10's 7: seven 1.6in heads plus a band edge cannot hold "Amazon Bedrock".
- Activation bars are named state.*: a bar encodes "executing", and at 0.10in it is a strip.
