# A1 kit-core: API and usage note

## Built
- **theme.py `accent-bright`**: a text role (large text only). Solved at load unless the theme JSON gives it:
  `accent1` when it clears `BRIGHT_MIN` 3.0 against `BRIGHT_AGAINST` (ink-deep, ink-strong, navy), else `accent1
  lumMod v lumOff 100000-v` (hue kept, lightened) by the least step that does. Three gated pairs in `CONTRAST_PAIRS`;
  `theme check` prints them and a `derived accent-bright lighten` line. A theme no step can solve (a light dk2) still
  loads and theme check FAILs the pair. Resolved: sigmoid F28986, neutral 8BA7E3, #0B6E4F client 12B17F.
- **prims**: `stat(..., basis=True)`, `stat_height(..., basis=True)`; `basis=False` draws no chip, alt text still
  `stat kind <kind>`. `prims.basis_label(kind)` returns "Illustrative figures" / "Target figures" / "Benchmark
  figures" / None.
- **compose.stat_band(slide, box, stats, *, fill="ink-deep", name, theme=None)** returns the stat names; drawn with
  `basis=False`; one label, exact text from `basis_label`, ink-muted 9pt, right-aligned above the band (shape
  `<name>.basis`), band below it. Mixed bases (delivered with illustrative, illustrative with target) raise
  GeometryError: split the band. Numerals on ink-deep, ink-strong or navy: `accent-ink` where it clears 3:1 on that
  fill (sigmoid red on ink-deep, 3.44, so the house look is unchanged), else `accent-bright`.
- **compose.icon_cards(..., spread=False)** and **compose.ask_cards(..., spread=False)** return the row foot y. Every
  card is as tall as the fullest stack and set at the box top. `spread=True` makes cards the box height and shares the
  slack over the stack's gaps; slack over `HOLLOW` (30%) of the box raises GeometryError. ask_cards now writes the
  numeral and verb in `<name>.N.mark` (stops 0.10in short of the glyph) and head and body in `<name>.N.text` below
  both: no glyph overlap (P05).
- **compose.numbered_rows**: row text frames hug their widest line (`_hug`), so one-line rows are not identical tiles.
- **compose.stage_chain**: slack between steps and facts is shared (fact rows open to 14pt, rest split above the
  glyphs and above the facts), never one hollow band. **pressure_band** docstring: top or middle, never the foot.
- **devices.place(prs, id, fill, *, theme, keywords=(), archetype=None, variant=None)**: raises GeometryError before
  adding a slide when the device serves more than one archetype and `archetype=` is missing. A filled title slot is
  set like `text.title()`: `canvas.title_box` (WIDE 0.50, 0.13, 12.40 x 0.40), `title_pt` 21, major face, two-tone,
  one line, 14 words (Overflows / OverBudget). It narrows only to stop 0.20in short of a device shape beside it
  (DL28's legend: width 7.53).
- **scripts/template_build.py**: what `new_deck.py` scaffolds. Three slides: executive-summary (two numbered_rows
  panels plus stat_band), layered-architecture `l0-house` from `deckkit.arch` (26 product marks), the-ask from device
  DL01 with `archetype="the-ask"`. All copy in one `COPY` dict between `# --- COPY ---` markers, as `{{placeholders}}`
  that P19 and P17 refuse. Filled with real copy it passes all 26 gates (tested).
- **scripts/new_deck.py**: `scaffold(name, out, theme, date=None)` swaps the skill path, name, deliverable, theme and
  today's date into the template; deck.json `"type": "deck"`. Refuses if the template drifted from its swap points.
- **example_epic.py**: s1 panels plus stat band, s2 pressure band at top plus stage chain with a Measure fact row (no
  foot band), s3 engine L0 (`layered-architecture:l0-ladder-rail`, 26 marks) plus the ladder rail. 26/26 gates on
  sigmoid, neutral and a #0B6E4F brand theme. **example_build.py**: foundation band now ink-strong, stats `basis=False`
  with one label; docstring points new decks at template_build.py.
- **tests/test_core_fixes.py**: 17 tests, including the scaffold filled and gated and the epic on three themes.

## How a builder uses it
- Stat rows: `compose.stat_band` or `prims.stat(..., basis=False)` plus one `basis_label(kind)` text on the slide. Card
  rows: place the next row at the returned foot. Devices: pass `archetype=` (21 of 31 serve more than one).
