# E1 arch base and topology: deckkit.arch extensions, P26 by type

Built in `kit/deckkit/arch.py` (public API backward compatible; every new argument is keyword with a default):
- `Diagram(..., gutter=None)`: air between top-level groups side by side (a peering or a labelled flow runs there).
- `group(..., fact=None, align_rows=False, lift=0.0)`: `fact` draws "label · fact" (spec 1.6); an outer dashed
  boundary without one is listed in `metrics()["advisories"]`, never raised. `align_rows` gives sibling child
  groups one set of row heights (AZ subnet tiers share one y). `lift` raises a frame's foot (twin of `drop`).
- `span(id, label, over, kind=None, *, fact=None, label_at=0, label_side="left")`: an overlay band across sibling
  columns (ECS service across AZ app subnets). Drawn as four `state.<nm>.span.<id>.<t|r|b|l>` lines plus the kind's
  tab and label in a strip each spanned group reserves. A flow may start or end on the span.
- `pin(id, "vendor:<key>", group, corner="tr", label=None)`: a 0.22in notation mark in a group corner (NACL, NSG).
  Named `*-pin`; not a product mark; carries no flow (P26.s). `tl` is refused (the label is there).
- `node(..., cols=1)`: the node's cell spans `cols` columns (an indented tree keeps a wide caption).
- `flow(src, dst, ..., hero=False, route=None)`: src and dst may be nodes, groups or spans (a group or span end
  lands on its frame edge, level with the other end where they overlap). Kinds, one prstDash each: data solid,
  async `dash`, control `sysDot`, reply `sysDash` 0.75pt (drawn 0.08in beside its request; declare the request
  first), peering `lgDashDot` (both heads, label required), feedback `lgDash` (perimeter route by default),
  blocked `dashDot` (no-entry glyph 0.16in before the target, keyed with the glyph), private `sysDashDot`
  (was sysDash; reply took it). `hero=True`: the primary path, 1.5pt in the emphasis role; hero flows must form
  one chain. `route="perimeter"|"perimeter-top"|"perimeter-bottom"` runs round the outside of everything between.
- `bus(id, srcs, dsts, label=None, kind="data", step=None, *, tree=False)`: one source to many or many to one;
  legs share the trunk (one line, one head where they merge). `tree=True`: the indented OU and account tree.
- `align(node, to, axis="y", offset=0.0)`: offset places a pin just above or below a node's line.
- `change(node, letter)` and module `change_key(slide, box, rows, theme, name, head=None, cols=1, spread=False)`:
  lettered change markers on both diagrams of a current versus target slide, and their before/after rail.
- Routing gains a vertical Z (down, across, down into a top). Labels are placed by measured fit (spec 1.5, E30):
  a segment must be the label's extent plus 0.12in, else the label is refused (draft mode lists it).
- `metrics()` adds `advisories`, `spans`, `pins`, `hero`, `changes`; `labelled` counts bus legs.

Gate P26 (`gates/p26_architecture.py`):
- Scope: layered-architecture, solution-architecture, and any variant starting `arch-`. The variant's words pick
  the spec 2 type (`diagram_type()`, `TYPES`, `TYPE_WORDS`): deploy/network/topology, cicd/release, mlops/llmops,
  dfd/threat, dataflow/medallion, lineage, seq/sequence, event, hub, dr/landing, current/target/asis/tobe,
  fsm/statemachine/state, c4/context/container, aws, azure. Unknown arch-* words get a NOTE and legacy rules.
- Per type: marks min and max, labelled share (deploy, cicd, dr count boundary-crossing flows, label or key
  only), steps band ('every' on DFD), boundary kinds, words (arch-* over the band fails).
- New checks: (g) double heads only on a labelled peering; (j) per-type steps; (k) per-type kinds; (l) nesting at
  most 4 on arch-*; (m) every dashed style keyed, at most 6 keys; (r) span and card exemptions (a mark inside a
  card or span a flow lands on is connected; C4 and seq heads); (s) pins carry no flow, deployment tiers share one
  y, spans cross two AZs, change letters mark both diagrams, a VPC crossing may carry a label instead of a pin;
  (u) label touching label and label on caption. Marks counted by alt record at any size (E29).
- Boundaries are not tiles: dashed, notation-stroked and Azure panel frames are excluded from (b) shells and from
  the tile-text test. Judgement call for the user: AWS subnets holding one icon were failing (b).
- 15 planters in `gates/negatives_arch.py`; `run_all --negatives --only P26`: 29 of 29 behave.
