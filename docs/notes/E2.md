# E2 arch flows: `deckkit.arch_flow`

Five flow diagram types, drawn natively in the house grammar beside `deckkit.arch` (arch.py untouched; its notation
constants, `_ink_box` and `_crop_to_ink` are read from it). Example: `scripts/example_arch_flow.py` (5 slides,
Quillmont canon, 27/27 gates pass incl. P26 at each arch-* type, P15, P21). Tests: `tests/test_arch_flow.py` (15).

## Type builders: content dict in, unrendered `Board` out
Nodes are tuples `(id, mark, name, role)`; marks `vendor:<key>` or `mark:<key>`. Step texts in path order; the builder
numbers the path. Then `metrics = board.render(slide)`.

| Call | Tag it | Anatomy (spec) |
|---|---|---|
| `cicd(box, th, spec, name=, draft=)` | process-flow:arch-cicd | 3.6: accounts as columns, gates with fail exits, digest rail, rollback loop, blocked console path |
| `mlops(...)` | solution-architecture:arch-mlops | 3.7: two zones in one frame, registry hinged on the divider, eval panel, approval, canary, online-eval perimeter loop |
| `medallion(...)` | layered-architecture:arch-medallion | 3.9: stacked sources, stream over batch, bronze/silver/gold cells, excluded record, band, lineage inset +N more |
| `events(...)` | convergence-hub:arch-events | 3.11 router: producers, event-type chips on an EventBridge bus, rule per type, schema registry, DLQ after 3 retries |
| `state_graph(...)` | decision-logic:arch-state | ruling 21: START/path/END, conditional edges dashed and labelled, retry, human_review interrupt, services above/below |

Spec keys are in each docstring; the example script is the worked instance of every key.
Variant naming: P26 types `arch-lineage` as the dataset-DAG view (0 to 10 marks). A platform board with a lineage
inset is dataflow: tag it `arch-medallion` (never put the word lineage in that variant).

## The board, for a custom anatomy
`Board(box, theme, cols=[weights], rows=[inches | "auto" | ("auto", w)], name=, keys={kind: legend text}, draft=)`
- `frame(id, kind, label, cols=(c0,c1), rows=(r0,r1), fact=, cells=, fill="card", icon="vendor:|mark:")`. Kinds:
  region (navy bar), boundary (dashed, label tab), zone (accent-deep dash), panel (card), band (`*.band`, P26.r
  exempt), section, segmented (cells `[label | (label, ncols)]`), lane (label only). The first row of a frame is its
  head row: `HEAD_BAR`, `HEAD_LABEL`, `HEAD_SECTION`.
- Nodes by cell `(col, row)`, `slot=(k,n)`, `span=(c0,c1)`, `align`, `hinge=c` (centred on a column line):
  `mark`, `gate(kind quality|approval|health|traffic, criterion)`, `stop`, `activity`, `state(interrupt=)`,
  `terminal`, `chip(kind event|dataset|token|more)`, `stack(entries, cell, rows)` (a list across rows).
- `edge(a, b, label, kind, step=, sides=(sa,sb), lane=("row", r)|("y", in), via=, ports=(oa, ob))`; kinds data,
  async, control, conditional, feedback (lgDash, accent-deep), blocked (no-entry glyph before the target).
- `bus(sources, target, label)` fan-in; `rail(id, label, row, cols, chips=[(id, text, col)])`; `steps({n: text})`.
- `render(slide, legend="auto")`: the board's last row must be free; it holds steps and the edge key.

## What it enforces (refuses, or lists under draft=True)
- Captions at most 2 lines per part, a name of 3 words or fewer on one line, labels one line and 4 words, 8pt floor.
- Nodes never overlap, stay on the board and 0.04in inside their frame; nested frames inset 0.12in; frames of one
  row share a foot line; an outer boundary or zone states a fact after a middot (P26.k).
- Routes: orthogonal, at most 2 bends, land on a node edge never its caption side, final leg 0.15in, never along a
  frame border or another edge, zero crossings (P26.h). Router tries straight, Z, L, C, U on every free side and port.
- Labels beside their segment on the local ground, wholly inside or outside a frame (a 0.08in poke only from
  outside), never where they read as a mark's caption; badges at the source end; steps 1..N.
- Flat fills, card radius 0.05in, no shadow, no left accent; marks cropped to their ink; corner marks named `*-tab`.
