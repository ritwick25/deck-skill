# A2 exhibits: deckkit.exhibits, deckkit.tables, scripts/capture.py

Components a proposal shows instead of describing. Each measures every word before drawing, raises (Overflows,
Capacity, GeometryError, BelowFloor, UnsupportedPart) instead of clamping, and runs as one transaction, so a refusal
leaves the slide unchanged. Roles only; vendor and brand marks keep their own artwork. Proof:
`scripts/example_exhibits.py` (six slides, all 26 implemented gates pass, every slide rendered and read) and
`tests/test_exhibits.py` (26 tests).

## Signatures (`from deckkit import exhibits, tables`)
- `exhibits.cycle_ring(slide, box, stages, hub=None, *, style="wedges"|"nodes", fill=None, hub_fill=None,
  active=None, start=None, label_w=None, name, theme=None) -> {"ring", "hub", "centre", "radius", "labels"}`
  stages `[(glyph, head, line)]` clockwise, 3 to 8. hub `"label"` or `("head", "line")`. wedges: ONE custGeom ring of
  arrow-pointed segments (point nests in the next notch); nodes: discs joined by curved headed arcs. `active` = the
  current stage in accent. Labels sit outside the ring's square at each stage's angle; the ring takes the largest
  radius at which they fit. lifecycle-loop.
- `exhibits.mark_wall(slide, box, groups, *, tab_fill="ink-strong", fill="card", caption_pt=10, max_mark=0.80,
  name, theme=None) -> {"groups", "mark", "rows"}`; groups `[(label, [(key, caption)])]`, key `vendor:<diagrams
  key>` | `mark:<registry key>` | `mono:<letters>`. A solid tab per group over a card; every mark over its caption;
  column width follows the group's marks (never an even tile grid, never pills); one row count and mark size for
  the wall, chosen to avoid empty rows. credentials, why-us, tech stacks.
- `exhibits.screen_frame(slide, box, png, chrome="browser"|"laptop"|None, band=None, *, pill=None, alt=,
  type_pt=None, crop_top=0, whole=False, name, theme=None) -> {"window", "screen", "type_pt"}`. Full box width;
  a taller capture is cropped at its foot, never shrunk (`whole=True` narrows instead). Browser: neutral ring dots
  and an address field with a lock glyph (no traffic-light colours). `band` = a solid role drawn as `bleed.band`
  across the slide, window at two thirds, `pill` on the band. The floor: reads the capture sidecar, converts the
  smallest CSS font to points at the placed width, BelowFloor under 8pt; no sidecar needs `type_pt=` (0 = no text).
  The figure goes in the picture's alt text and the return value (put it in the manifest). product-screen.
- `exhibits.portrait(slide, box, image=None, initials=None, *, fill="ink-deep", alt=None, name, theme=None)`:
  a PNG/JPEG centre-cropped and clipped to an ellipse (192 dpi and alt text required), or an initials disc. Neither
  raises: never a generic silhouette.
- `exhibits.quote_portrait(slide, box, text, attribution, *, image=None, initials=None, alt=None,
  portrait_fill="ink-deep", fill="card", portrait_in=None, name, theme=None) -> Box`: portrait left, prims.quote
  right (its state.quote bar), the panel as tall as its words, both centred.
- `exhibits.cell_grid(slide, box, rows, cols, cells, legend, *, key=None, row_w=None, gap=0.06, name, theme=None)
  -> {"cells", "header", "rail"}`. rows `[label | (label, line)]` on a near-black rail, cols under ink-strong tabs,
  cells a state key or `(key, word)`; legend `{key: (glyph, word, fill)}`, fill accent | accent-deep | ink-deep |
  ink-strong | navy | card | paper. Every cell is glyph plus word; the key row draws when a cell overrides its word.
  coverage-matrix.
- `tables.table(slide, box, header, rows, marked_col=None, cell_glyphs=None, *, widths=None, pt=11,
  header_fill="ink-strong", first_col_bold=True, fill_height=True, align=None, name, theme=None)
  -> (graphicFrame, height, row_tops, col_lefts)`. Promoted from tests/build_golden.py: No Style No Grid, explicit
  margins and fills, one hairline under each body row. Rows measured, then share the box height; Overflows past
  it; a word wider than its column refuses. `marked_col`: accent header, bold body on card (options-comparison).
  `cell_glyphs={"Yes": "circle-check", "No": ("circle-x", "ink-muted")}`: glyph laid over the cell, word kept.
- `scripts/capture.py page.html out.png [--width 1280 --height 800 --scale 2 --placed-width 11.5 --full-page
  --selector CSS]` or `capture.capture(...)`: Playwright 2x PNG plus `<stem>.capture.json` (css size, scale, smallest
  font px and where, placed width, min_pt, png sha256). Refuses under 8pt at the placed width, scale under 2, and a
  page with external requests; writes nothing when it refuses.

## Builder use
Size the box from the card's zones, call the component, gate, read. Capture once into the project (P21 rebuilds
byte-for-byte); put mark sha256s in deck.json `media_allow` (the example's `mark_media()`).
